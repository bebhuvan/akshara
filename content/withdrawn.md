A few books that were once here have been taken down, because on a closer look they, or parts of the editions they were made from, are still in copyright in India, where a book enters the public domain sixty years after its author's death.

- *Foreign Notices of South India*, edited by K. A. Nilakanta Sastri, who died in 1975.
- *The Life and Letters of Raja Rammohun Roy*, in the 1962 edition by Biswas and Ganguli. Sophia Dobson Collet's own text of 1900 is in the public domain, and may return here when it is set again from an early printing.
- The introduction by J. P. Guha to *A Comprehensive History of India*, volume I, which was written for a modern reprint. The rest of that book remains in [the library](/books/comprehensive-history-india-v1/).

If you think another book here should not be, please [write to me](/contact/).
