The Bengal Renaissance was a cultural, social, intellectual, and artistic movement that took place in Bengal during the 19th and early 20th centuries. This period saw the emergence of modern Indian thought, literature, and cross-cultural scholarship.

Through works such as *Life and Letters of Toru Dutt*, this collection illuminates remarkable Bengali writers and thinkers who bridged Indian cultural traditions and international letters.
