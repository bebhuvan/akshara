<span id="page-5" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 class="sec-h2">No. 2.</h3><h4 class="sec-h3"><span class="sc">Announcement dated 2nd February 1920 issued by the Government of India.</span><sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-03-fn-001" href="#sec-03-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-03-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup></h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The acquisition rate for gold imported under licence into India, which has hitherto been subject to variation notified from time to time, has now, in accordance with the Secretary of State for India's separate announcement published to-day relating to the <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-6" data-page="6" data-folio="4" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/pamphletsissuedb05grea/page/n5_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 4">[p. 4]</button> recommendations of the Indian Currency Committee, been fixed, and the following fixed rates will apply to transactions on and after Monday, 2nd February, namely, Rs. 10 for each sovereign tendered for import, or one rupee for 11·30016 grains of fine gold.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">2. Council drafts will continue to be offered at the Secretary of State's discretion for weekly sale at the Bank of England by competitive tender. The rate for deferred telegraphic transfers and bills will, until further notice, rank for allotment with tenders at <sup>1</sup>/<sub>16</sub>th of a penny higher for immediate telegraphic transfers. No announcement will be made of the minimum rate at which tenders will be accepted, and the Secretary of State for India in Council reserves the right of rejecting the whole or part of any tender. In accordance with the Committee's recommendations, the Government of India will, when occasion requires, offer for sale stated weekly amounts of sterling reverse drafts on the Secretary of State for India (including immediate telegraphic transfers).</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The rates for immediate telegraphic transfers on London will be announced on each occasion by the Controller of Currency, and will be based on the sterling equivalent of the price of 11·30016 grains fine gold, as measured by the prevailing sterling-dollar exchange, less a deduction representing the charges of remitting gold. The rate for deferred drafts on London will, until further notice, be <sup>1</sup>/<sub>16</sub>th of a penny higher than the immediate rate as at present.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">3. The Finance Department Notification No. 4071<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-03-fn-002" href="#sec-03-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-03-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup>, dated the 11th September 1897, providing for the issue of rupees at the reserve treasuries in Calcutta, Madras, and Bombay, in exchange for sovereigns and half-sovereigns at the rate of Rs. 15 and Rs. 7½ respectively, is cancelled. Notification No. 6908A<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-03-fn-002-2" href="#sec-03-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-03-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup>, dated the 11th December 1906, regarding the receipt of sovereigns and half-sovereigns at the mints, is also cancelled.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">4. The existing prohibition on the import of silver is cancelled, and the import duty of four annas an ounce is abolished. The prohibition on the export of silver remains.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">5. The Notifications under the Defence of India Act<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-03-fn-002-3" href="#sec-03-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-03-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> prohibiting the use of gold and silver coin otherwise than as currency, or dealing therein at a premium, are cancelled.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-03-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-03-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">The first two paragraphs of the above Announcement were also issued in London on 2nd Feb. 1920.</span></li><li id="sec-03-fn-002" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-03-fn-002" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">See Appendix.</span></li></ol></section>
