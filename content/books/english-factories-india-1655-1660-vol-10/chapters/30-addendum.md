<span id="page-422" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">While this work was in the press, Sir Richard Temple kindly communicated, from papers in the possession of the Master family, some further particulars regarding the early career of Streynsham Master. In 1655 he ‘went to schoole at London to writ and cipher to Mr. Thomas Fox’. The date of his departure for India in the <em>Smyrna Merchant</em> was 4 April, 1656. In October, 1657, he sailed from Surat in the <em>Dove</em>, with Colonel Rainsford, to Rājāpur and Cannanore, returning in the following December. Blake, the captain of the vessel, died and was buried at Rājāpur. He was succeeded by Jeremy Weddell (son of the famous John Weddell), ‘and the ship goeing to Legorne never heard of’. During 1658 Master lay ill for many months, apparently at Surat.</p></div>
