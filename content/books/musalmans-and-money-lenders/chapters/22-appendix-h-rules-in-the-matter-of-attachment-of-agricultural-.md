<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-210"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="210" data-folio="198" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924013743848/page/n209_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 198">[p. 198]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">APPENDIX H.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-22-fn-001" href="#sec-22-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-22-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup></h2><h3 class="sec-h2">RULES IN THE MATTER OF ATTACHMENT OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE IN DECREES FOR DEBT IN THE KAPURTHALA STATE.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Attachment of the produce of the land or of cattle in execution of decreeagainst an agriculturist shall only be granted at harvest time.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">2. At harvest time the court may attach—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>a</em>). Any surplus produce of former harvests belonging to the judgment-debtor.</li><li>(<em>b</em>). Surplus cattle not necessary for tilling the ground.</li><li>(<em>c</em>). A fixed share of the produce of the current harvest.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">3. The fixed share mentioned in the previous rule shall not exceed—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>a</em>). From owners and tenants who pay revenue or rent in cash, two-thirds of the grain and one-third of the straw in ordinary crops and three-fourths of rabi crops: provided that the judgment-creditor pays into court the whole of the revenue or rent charges due on the crop.</li><li>(<em>b</em>). From tenants who pay rent in kind, one-half of the tenant's share of the grain in ordinary crops and three-fifths of the tenant's share in rabi crops.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">4. Sums paid into court as revenue under the previous rule shall be credited to the State on account of the crops to which they relate in the judgment-debtor's behalf, and the court shall give notice of such payments to the Collector, who shall give such directions concerning them as he shall see fit.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">5. Sums paid into court under Rule 3 as rent charges shall be made over to the judgment-debtor so soon as the report of the attachment of the produce has been received from the attaching officer.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-22-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-22-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">Accompaniments to Punjab Government printed letter No. 459 S., dated 17th September, 1880.</span></li></ol></section>
