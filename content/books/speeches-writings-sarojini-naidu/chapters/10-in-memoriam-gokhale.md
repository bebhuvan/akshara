<span id="page-67" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 class="sec-h1">IN MEMORIAM: GOKHALE</h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>This touching tribute to the memory of Gokhale, approved under Gokhale’s portrait in Mr. (now Sir) Dinshaw Wacha’s brochure on the great man :</em></p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Heroic heart ! lost hope of all our days !</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Need’st thou the homage of our love or praise ?</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Lo ! let the mournful millions round thy pyre</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Kindle their souls with consecrated fire</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Caught from the brave torch fallen from thy hand,</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">To succour and to save our stricken land ;</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">And in a daily worship taught by thee</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Upbuild the Temple of her Unity.</div></div>
