<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-83"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="83" data-folio="45" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/speecheswritings00naiduoft/page/n82_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 45">[p. 45]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Mrs. Sarojini Naidu indited the following verses for the Report of the Hyderabad Ladies’ War Relief Association, December</em> 1915:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">Is there aught you need that my hands withhold,</span><br>
<span class="line">Rich gifts of raiment or grain or gold?</span><br>
<span class="line">Lo! I have flung to the East and West</span><br>
<span class="line">Priceless treasures torn from my breast,</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">And yielded the sons of my stricken womb</span><br>
<span class="line">To the drum beats of duty, the sabres of doom.</span><br>
<span class="line">Gathered like pearls in their alien graves</span><br>
<span class="line">Silent they sleep by the Persian waves,</span><br>
<span class="line">Scattered like shells on Egyptian sands,</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">They lie with pale brows and brave, broken hands,</span><br>
<span class="line">They are strewn like blossoms mown down by chance</span><br>
<span class="line">On the blood-brown meadows of Flanders and France.</span><br>
<span class="line">Can ye measure the grief of the tears I weep</span><br>
<span class="line">Or compass the woe of the watch I keep?</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">Or the pride that thrills thro’ my heart’s despair,</span><br>
<span class="line">And the hope that comforts the anguish of prayer?</span><br>
<span class="line">And the far sad and glorious vision I see</span><br>
<span class="line">Of the torn red banners of Victory?</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">When the terror and tumult of hate shall cease</span><br>
<span class="line">And life be refashioned on anvils of peace,</span><br>
<span class="line">And your love shall offer memorial thanks</span><br>
<span class="line">To the comrades who fought in your dauntless ranks,</span><br>
<span class="line">And you honour the deeds of the deathless ones</span><br>
<span class="line">Remember the blood of thy martyred sons!</span></div></div>
