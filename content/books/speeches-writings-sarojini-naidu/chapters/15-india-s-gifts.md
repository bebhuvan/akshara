<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-83"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="83" data-folio="45" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/speecheswritings00naiduoft/page/n82_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 45">[p. 45]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">INDIA'S GIFTS</h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Mrs. Sarojini Naidu indited the following verses for the Report of the Hyderabad Ladies' War Relief Association, December</em> 1915:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Is there aught you need that my hands withhold,<br>
Rich gifts of raiment or grain or gold ?<br>
Lo ! I have flung to the East and West<br>
Priceless treasures torn from my breast,</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">And yielded the sons of my stricken womb<br>
To the drum beats of duty, the sabres of doom.<br>
Gathered like pearls in their alien graves<br>
Silent they sleep by the Persian waves,<br>
Scattered like shells on Egyptian sands,</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">They lie with pale brows and brave, broken hands,<br>
They are strewn like blossoms mown down by chance<br>
On the blood-brown meadows of Flanders and France.<br>
Can ye measure the grief of the tears I weep<br>
Or compass the woe of the watch I keep ?</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Or the pride that thrills thro' my heart's despair,<br>
And the hope that comforts the anguish of prayer ?<br>
And the far sad and glorious vision I see<br>
Of the torn red banners of Victory ?</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">When the terror and tumult of hate shall cease<br>
And life be refashioned on anvils of peace,<br>
And your love shall offer memorial thanks<br>
To the comrades who fought in your dauntless ranks,<br>
And you honour the deeds of the deathless ones<br>
Remember the blood of thy martyred sons !</div></div>
