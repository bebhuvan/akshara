Who kneel in thy presence to serve and adore thee!<br>
The night is aflush with a dream of the morrow,<br>
Why still dost thou sleep in thy bondage of sorrow?<br>
Awaken and sever the woes that enthral us,<br>
And hallow our hands for the triumphs that call us!<br>
Are we not thine, O Belov'd, to inherit<br>
The manifold pride and power of thy spirit?<br>
Ne'er shall we fail thee, forsake thee or falter,<br>
Whose hearts are thy home and thy shield and thine<br>
altar,</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Lo! we would thrill the high stars with thy story,<br>
And set thee again in the forefront of glory.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Hindus:—Mother! the flowers of our worship have<br>
crowned thee!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Parsis:—Mother! the flame of our hope shall<br>
surround thee!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Mussalmans:—Mother! the sword of our love shall<br>
defend thee!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Christians:—Mother! the song of our faith shall<br>
attend thee!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">All Creeds:—Shall not our dauntless devotion avail<br>
thee? Hearken! O queen and O goddess, we<br>
hail thee!</div></div>
