This (the third) edition practically brings the collection up-to-date, including as it does her speeches in East and South Africa and in the Congress at Belgaum. The subjects treated cover a wide field—politics, education, social reform, Hindu-Muslim Unity, Satyagraha, Non-Co-operation, the position of Indians abroad—in fact every aspect of India’s problems is discussed in these pages with characteristic ardour and eloquence.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The biographical sketch of her life and career with which the volume opens and the index at the end will, it is hoped, be appreciated.</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-11"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="11" data-folio="v" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/speecheswritings00naiduoft/page/n10_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page v">[p. v]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">CONTENTS.</h2><p class="printed-toc-line">Mrs. Sarojini Naidu: A Sketch .. i</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Nilambuja: The Fantasy of a Poet’s Mood .. 1</p><p class="printed-toc-line">True Brotherhood .. 5</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Personal Element in Spiritual Life .. 8</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Education of Indian Women .. 11</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Mrs. Gandhi .. 14</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Reminiscences of Mr. Gokhale .. 17</p><p class="printed-toc-line">In Memoriam: Gokhale .. 29</p><p class="printed-toc-line">The Children’s Tribute to Gokhale .. 30</p><p class="printed-toc-line">The Ideal of Civic Life .. 34</p><p class="printed-toc-line">The Sunlit Lamps of India .. 41</p><p class="printed-toc-line">Sir P.
