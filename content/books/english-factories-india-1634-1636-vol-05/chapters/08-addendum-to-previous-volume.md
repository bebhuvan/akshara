<span id="page-401" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 class="sec-h2">ADDENDUM TO PREVIOUS VOLUME.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Note should have been taken of two manuscripts in the British Museum, viz. <em>Additional MS.</em> 18649, which contains copies of a journal of the voyages of the <em>Mary</em>, from February 2, 1631, to November 28, 1632; and <em>Sloane MS.</em> 3492, which records the voyage of Weddell’s fleet to India, etc., in 1632, ending with the arrival of the <em>Dolphin</em> at Bantam in January, 1634. The latter volume also contains a voyage from Bantam to Jambi in the <em>Dove </em> (March–May, 1634), and a portion of the homeward voyage of the <em>Hart</em> from Bantam in 1635.
