There is a register of the correspondence from 1802 to 1834.</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">LETTERS FROM THE COMPANY TO THE BOARD.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">1784–1858 (27 vols., copies, mostly indexed). 1817–27 (copies of enclosures, 1 vol.).</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">CORRESPONDENCE BETWEEN THE COMPANY AND THE BOARD.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">1784–95; 1801–23 (3 vols.). It relates to drafts in all departments. 1823–36 (1 vol.).<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-06-fn-004" href="#sec-06-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-06-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup> Compiled at the Board of Control, and relating solely to Secret and Political drafts. After 1836 the collection was merged in the next series.</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">SECRET AND POLITICAL CORRESPONDENCE.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-06-fn-004-2" href="#sec-06-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-06-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup></h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">1831–58 (20 vols., indexed). These are copies of letters received and sent. 1839–58 (52 vols., indexed). Original letters received, with drafts of the replies.</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">THE BOARD’S LETTER BOOKS.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">1784–1858 (20 vols., indexed). These contain copies of letters despatched by the Board; also, from 1840, copies of letters received.</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">“BOARD OF CONTROL LETTERS, &amp;c.”</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">1854--58 (12 vols.). Original letters received, with draft replies. Madras, 1814–27 (3 vols.). Bombay, 1814--27 (4 vols.).</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-56"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="56" data-folio="36" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/guidetoindiaoffi00greauoft/page/n55_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 36">[p. 36]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">“BOARD’S COLLECTIONS.” 1796–97 <span class="sc">to</span> 1857–58 (2,730 vols.).</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">These contain the papers on which the various drafts to all Presidencies were founded, including the enclosures to the letters to which the drafts replied. They were forwarded to the Board with the “Previous Communications,” returned and sent again with the “Drafts,” and were then retained by the Board. Collections of earlier date than 1796 were destroyed many years ago, and there are many gaps in the existing series, caused by the destruction of unimportant collections. There is a series of Registers of Board’s Collections in 18 vols., extending from 1794–95 to 1858.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The Political Department has in its custody several other volumes received from the Board of Control, dealing with political subjects.
