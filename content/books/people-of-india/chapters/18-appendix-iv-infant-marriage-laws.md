<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-467"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="467" data-folio="403" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924024114773/page/n466_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 403">[p. 403]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">APPENDIX IV</h2><h3 class="sec-h2">INFANT MARRIAGE LAWS, MYSORE AND BARODA</h3><h4 class="sec-h3"><strong>REGULATION No. X OF 1894.</strong></h4><h4 class="sec-h3"><span class="sc">(Passed on the 5th day of October, 1894.)</span></h4><h4 class="sec-h3"><em>A Regulation to prevent Infant Marriages in the Territories of Mysore.</em></h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Preamble.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">Whereas</span> it is expedient to prevent Infant Marriages in the Territories of Mysore: His Highness the Maharajah is pleased to enact as follows:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Short title.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">1. This Regulation may be called “The Mysore Infant Marriages Prevention Regulation.”</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Extent and commencement.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">(2) It shall extend to the whole of the territories of Mysore, but it shall apply only to marriages among the Hindus. It shall come into operation at the expiration of six months from the date of its publication in the official Gazette.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Definition.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">2. For the purposes of this Regulation, an “Infant Girl” means a girl who has not completed eight years of age.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Punishment for causing or abetting infant marriages.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">3. Any person who causes the marriage of an infant girl, or who knowingly aids and abets within the meaning of the Indian Penal Code such a marriage, and any man who having completed eighteen years of age marries an infant girl, shall be punished with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine, or with both.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Punishment for a man more than fifty years of age marrying a girl under fourteen years of age.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">4. Any man who having completed fifty years of age marries a girl who has not completed fourteen years of age, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Punishment for abetment of offence provided against in section 4.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">5. Any person who causes the marriage of a girl who has not completed fourteen years of age, with a man who has completed fifty years of age, and any person who knowingly aids and abets, within the meaning of the Indian Penal Code, such a marriage, shall be punished with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine, or with both.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Offences under the Regulation by whom triable.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">6. No offence punishable under this Regulation shall be tried by any Court inferior to that of a Magistrate of the District.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Validity of marriages notwithstanding the penalties provided by the Regulation.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">7. No marriage which has actually taken place, shall be deemed to be invalid, on the ground of the penalties provided by this Regulation.</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-468"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="468" data-folio="404" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924024114773/page/n467_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 404">[p. 404]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">No prosecution to be instituted without the previous sanction of Government.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">8. No prosecution under this Regulation shall be instituted without the previous written sanction of the Government accorded after such enquiry as the Government may deem fit to make.</p></div><h2 class="sec-h1">ABSTRACT TRANSLATION OF <strong>ACT No. VII OF SAMVAT 1960.</strong> <span class="sc">PASSED BY THE BARODA DURBAR.</span></h2><h3 class="sec-h2"><em>(Received the assent of His Highness the Maharajah Sahib on Ashad Sudi 2nd, i.e., the 15th of July, 1904.)</em></h3><h3 class="sec-h2">THE EARLY MARRIAGE PREVENTION ACT.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Preamble.</aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">Whereas</span> it is expedient to draw the increased attention of the public towards physical training, whereby the future progeny may be healthy and long-lived, His Highness the Gaekwar has been pleased to rule as under:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Title.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">1. This Act shall be called “The Early Marriage Prevention Act.”</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Commencement.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">2. This Act shall come into force on Ashad Sudi 9th Samvat 1960, <em>i.e.</em>, the 21st July, 1904.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Definitions.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">3. In this Code, the following words have the following meanings:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(1) “Minor” means—</li><li>(<em>a</em>) In case of a girl, one who has not completed her 12th year of age.</li><li>(<em>b</em>) In case of a boy, one who has not completed his 16th year.</li><li>(2) “Early marriage” means the marriage of a minor girl or of a minor boy. The word “marriage” does not include a “Baybar” marriage or a marriage with a ball of flowers, or such other nominal marriages.</li><li>(3) “Nyayadhish” means Nyayadhish of a Mahal, or who may be from time to time invested with the powers (of that officer).</li><li>(4) The term “Wahivatdar” includes Mahalkari also.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">What authorities of the State are to be applied to for permission to allow an early marriage.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">4. Whoever wishes to marry his minor son or daughter, or the minor girl or boy under his guardianship, shall submit a petition in writing to the Nyayadhish of the Taluka where he may be residing, or to that of the Taluka in which the intended marriage is to take place, for permission to allow the marriage.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Explanation.</em>—There is no objection to a joint petition being submitted by both the parties in cases in which the girl and the boy are both minors.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">The value of stamp on the application.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">5. The petitions alluded to in the preceding Section must be written on a (Court-fee) stamped paper of Rs. 2.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Particulars to be embodied in the petition.</aside></div><p class="prose-p">6. The petition mentioned in Section 4 must contain the following particulars:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>a</em>) the names, age, and caste of the bride and the bridegroom;</li><li>(<em>b</em>) the names of their father or guardian;</li><li>(<em>c</em>) the date and place of the marriage, and</li></ul></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-469"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="469" data-folio="405" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924024114773/page/n468_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 405">[p. 405]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>d</em>) the mention, in full, of the difficulties likely to crop up in case the marriage does not take place.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Procedure to be followed on receipt of the application.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">7. The Nyayadhish, on receipt of the application, shall take steps as under:—</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>a</em>) he shall fix a date, within the period of 15 days, for the hearing of the petition;</li><li>(<em>b</em>) shall appoint three gentlemen of the petitioner’s caste, as assessors; and</li><li>(<em>c</em>) shall issue summonses for their appearance in the Court, on the appointed date.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Enquiries in connection with the application.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">8. On the date of hearing, the Nyayadhish, assisted by the assessors, shall enquire into the petition, recording all such evidence as may be produced in its support, and shall dispose of the same by the end of the day.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(2) If the Nyayadhish and all the assessors present or any two of them be satisfied that in the event of the marriage not taking place on the appointed date,</li><li>(<em>a</em>) there is no probability of its celebration within one year after the bride or the bridegroom comes to age or that it will not take place at all, or</li><li>(<em>b</em>) it is likely that the parents or the guardian of the girl whose marriage is to be celebrated by them will not, owing to old age or infirmity, survive till she comes to age, and there is none else fit to take care of her besides them, or</li><li>(<em>c</em>) that unavoidable difficulties of a like nature are likely to crop up, the Nyayadhish shall grant written permission for the celebration of the marriage under his own seal and signature in accordance with the form laid down in the first schedule annexed to this Act.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Explanation.</em>—The permission to be granted for reasons mentioned in sub-sections (<em>b</em>) and (<em>c</em>) should only be granted if the girl’s age is above nine years.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(3) If permission for the celebration of an early marriage of a girl whose betrothal has taken place before this Act comes into force, be asked within three months after the passing thereof, the Nyayadhish should grant the necessary permission after satisfying himself only that the betrothal has taken place before this Act came into force.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Physical examination not compulsory.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">9. In course of an enquiry held, or investigations made, for the purposes of this Act, neither the bride nor the bridegroom will be compelled to be present for bodily examination or the bride for any other examination.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Procedure to be followed when the Court does not agree with the assessors.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">10. (<em>a</em>) If the Nyayadhish does not agree with the assessors or with any two of them, the proceedings shall be referred with their opinions and reasons therefor, by the Nyayadhish to the District Judge.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">(<em>b</em>) The District Judge shall dispose of the same within a week, and if he thinks that permission should be given, the same shall be given by him in writing as laid down in Section 8.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>The Suba to be supplied with a copy of the document granting the permission.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">11. The Nyayadhish granting the permission shall, within eight days, forward to the Suba of the District, a copy of the document by which the permission applied for was granted by him.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Punishment.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">12. Whoever celebrates or aids to celebrate an early marriage, which has not been given the required permission as laid down in Sections 8 and 10, shall be punished with a fine which may not exceed Rs. 100.</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-470"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="470" data-folio="406" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924024114773/page/n469_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 406">[p. 406]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Exception</em>—This provision does not extend to the bride and the bridegroom.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Period of time during which the offence can be tried.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">13. None of the offences provided for by this Act shall be tried by the Court, after two years from the date of the marriage.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>The registration of marriages.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">14. In all the towns, kasbas and villages of the Baroda State, the officials mentioned below shall keep a register of marriages as per the Schedule No. 2 and fill in the particulars as required by Section 15:</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><ul class="editorial-list"><li>(<em>a</em>) In the city of Baroda ... ... ... The Municipal Inspectors.</li><li>(<em>b</em>) In the kasbas ... ... ... ... The Sudhrai Kamdar.</li><li>(<em>c</em>) In the villages or kasbas where there is no municipality. Village or villages administered in any other way ... ... The Mulki Patel.</li><li>(<em>d</em>) In Inam village or villages administered in any other way ... ... ... The Mukhi, or Inamdar, or Administrator.</li></ul></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">(2) A copy of the registration (sub-section 1) shall be submitted before the 10th of the next month by the officials concerned to the Suba, through the Taluka Wahivatdar. Immediate information regarding marriages celebrated against the provision of this Act, shall be given to the Nyayadhish having jurisdiction.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">(3) The Suba on receipt of the copy (sub-section 2) shall submit annually a statement (Schedule No. 3) to the Sar Suba. It should be despatched within two months after the expiration of each year.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">(4) In the absence of any other orders, the Nyayadhish, to whom a copy of the register of marriages is sent according to sub-section 2, should understand that a complaint has been laid before him according to Section 12 of this Act and shall proceed to try the offence summarily in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure. The case, however, should not be handed over to the Police for investigations under Section 56 of the Criminal Procedure Code.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">(5) At the termination of the enquiries (sub-section 4) if any punishment be awarded, the same shall be communicated (as per Schedule No. 4) to the Suba within eight days.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>All marriages must be reported.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">15. The father or the guardian of the bride shall report the marriage to the officials concerned within eight days after its celebration and shall obtain from them particulars, as per the Schedule No. 5.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Punishment for not getting the marriages registered.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">16. If the father or the guardian of the bride fails to give the information about the marriage as required by the preceding section, or gives false information in connection with the same, he shall be punished with a fine which may not exceed Rs. 10.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>Responsibilities in connection with the registration of marriage.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">17. The responsibilities for the proper registration of marriages, as laid down in Section 14, shall rest with the Wahivatdar of the Mahal.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote"><strong>The marriage cannot be set aside.</strong></aside></div><p class="prose-p">18. An early marriage, if once celebrated, cannot be set aside.</p></div>
