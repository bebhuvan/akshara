This incidence is very heavy. The crop experiment reports also show that the incidence of taxation in these districts must be very heavy. In the report for 1897-98 we notice that out of nine experiments made in the Kaira district, one showed 72 per cent., and another 67 per cent., as the incidence of the assessment on the gross value of the produce. In Broach 31 experiments are reported in that year. One of these shows the incidence at 42 per cent., and in six it was over 30 per cent. In the report for 1896-97, we find nine experiments. One of them gives the incidence at 96 per cent., one at 73 per cent., one at 63 per cent.. and one at 50 per cent.”</p></blockquote></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Mr Gokuldas Parakh's Presidential Speech at the Provincial Conference, at Satara, May</em> 1900.</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-348"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="348" data-folio="324" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/cu31924022938264/page/n347_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 324">[p. 324]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="imprint-line">PRINTED BY
