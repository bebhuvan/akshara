<em>If we are true to ourselves and to our country and</em><br>
<em>make all the necessary sacrifices for our elevation</em><br>
<em>and amelioration, I, for one, have not the shadow of</em><br>
<em>a doubt that in dealing with such justice-loving,</em><br>
<em>fair-minded people as the British, we may rest</em><br>
<em>fully assured that we shall not work in vain. It</em><br>
<em>is this conviction which has supported me against</em><br>
<em>all difficulties. I have never faltered in my faith</em><br>
<em>in the British character and have always believed</em><br>
<em>that the time will come when the sentiments of the</em><br>
<em>British Nation and our Gracious Sovereign proclaimed to us in our Great Charter of the Proclamation of</em> 1858 <em>will be realised, (applause),</em><br>

<em>their contentment our best reward.” And let us</em><br>
<em>join in the prayer that followed this hopeful declaration of our Sovereign: “May the God of all</em>-<em>power grant to us and to those in authority under</em><br>
<em>us strength to carry out these our wishes for the</em><br>

<em>Address to the Lahore Congress.</em></p></blockquote></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-14"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="14" data-folio="viii" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/speecheswritings00naor/page/n13_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page viii">[p. viii]</button></div><h3 class="sec-h2">DADABHAI'S EXHORTATION.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><blockquote><p><em>My last prayer and exhortation to the Congress and to all my countrymen is—Go on united and earnest, in concord and harmony, with moderation, with loyalty to the British rule and patriotism towards our country, and success is sure to attend our efforts for our just demands, and the day, I hope, is not distant when the world will see the noblest spectacle of a great nation like the British holding out the hand of true fellow-citizenship and of justice to the vast mass of humanity of this great and ancient land of India with benefits and blessings to the human race (loud and prolonged cheering).—From the Presidential Address to the Lahore Congress.</em></p></blockquote></div>
