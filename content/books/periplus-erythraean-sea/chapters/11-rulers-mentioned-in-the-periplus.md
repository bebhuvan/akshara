Zoscales, king of the people called Axumites. (Dates fixed by Salt in 1804 as 76-89 A. D.; his conclusions, depending on an arbitrary arrangement of the Abyssinian Chronicle, as he said himself, are "not to be depended upon;" a more probable period for this reign would be 59-72 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 19. Malichas, king of the Nabatæans. (Mentioned also by Josephus, <em>Bell. Iud.</em>, III, 4, 2. Inscriptions cited by Vogüé fix his dates as 40-70 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 23. Charibael, king of the Homerites and Sabaites. (Inscriptions cited by Glaser fix his reign about 40-70 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 23. The Emperors. (Probably Claudius and Nero, 41-54 and 54-68 respectively.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 27. Eleazus, king of the Frankincense Country. (Inscriptions cited by Glaser fix his reign about 25-65 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 38. Parthian princes at war with each other. (Probably within the decade following the death of Gondophares, which occurred 51 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 41. Nambanus, king of Ariaca. (Perhaps Nahapāna, the Saka satrap—or a predecessor of that name—but <em>before</em> the victories which led to the establishment of the Saka era of 78 A. D.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 52. The elder Saraganus, who had previously governed Calliena. (Probably Arishta Sātākarni, then the Andhra king, who ruled about 44-69 A. D.; whose court was held at his eastern capital, Dhānyakataka, so that to the author of the Periplus, landing on the west coast, he was no more than a name, and the visible authority was vested in the western viceroy.)</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">§ 52. Sandares, who possessed Calliena. (Probably Sundara Sātākarni—who ruled as Andhra king in 83-4 A. D.—but <em>before</em> his accession to the throne, while as one of the heirs presumptive he was acting as viceroy at Pæthana, toward the end of the reign of Arishta Sātākarni, the "elder Saraganus.")</p></div>
