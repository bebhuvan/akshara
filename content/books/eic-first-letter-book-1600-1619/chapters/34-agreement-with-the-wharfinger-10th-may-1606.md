<span id="page-181" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 class="sec-h2">THE AGREEMENT w<sup>th</sup> the Wharffenger.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">The agreem<sup>t</sup> w<sup>th</sup> the wharfenger.</aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">His Byll</span> Wittnesseth. That yt is couenanted concluded &amp; agreed betweene Thomas Watson wharfenger of Custome howse &amp; woll keys, &amp; the Comitties for the East India Companie in forme followinge. viz. That the said Comitties, doe promyse to pay vnto the said Tho. Watson or his assignes for lighteridge wharffedg and Cranadge all &amp; other thingꬴ necessarie to this effect the some of 3<sup>d</sup> for euʳy bagg baile Canister hoggeshead or Caske. In Considerac̃on whereof the said <strong>T W.</strong> doth for himselfe &amp; his Assignes Couenaunte p̄myse &amp; agree to and w<sup>th</sup> the said Comitties That he the said <strong>T: W:</strong> att his owne p̄p Coste &amp; chardges shall finde sufficient stanch &amp; stronge lighters and able Craines and men for carryinge &amp; Crayinge of the said Companies goodes now to be vnladen out of their shipps latelie arriued in the Riuer of Thames, And furder to satisfie the said Companie for whatsoeuʳ damadge shall happen vnto the same goodes by the insufficiencie of any th lighters or Craines, Crayne Ropes or slynge he shall sett a worke or by the default or vnfaithfullnes of any p̄son whatsoeuʳ he shall ymploye aboute the same lighters Craines or wharffes or any of them. And further to carrie abourd the said Companies shipps in his said lighters all such baggꬴ emptie Caske &amp; other thinge as shall be needfull to be imployed for the same goodꬴ w<sup>th</sup>out demaunding any thinge for the same. And to Conclude it is furder agreed <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-182" data-page="182" data-folio="90" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n181_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 90">[p. 90]</button> by &amp; betwene the said p̄ties, that yf att any tyme in the p̄formance of the premisses the said <strong>𝔗. 𝔚.</strong> shall be found to deale otherwyse then shall be fittinge for the good of the Companie: Or yf any other will serue the Companie in this respect from blackwall better cheepe then is formerlie agreed vpon That then and in such case it shalbe free for the said Comitties to make choyse of others, or to ymploie the said <strong>𝔗. 𝔚.</strong> still att their will and pleasures vnto the p̄formance whereof we doe binde o<sup>r</sup> selues by this o<sup>r</sup> present writtinge. And in Witnes of the same, we haue therevnto sett o<sup>r</sup> hande the 10<sup>th</sup> of May 1606.</p></div>
