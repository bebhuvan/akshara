Wheareas o<span class="sc"><sup>r</sup></span> late deere sister Queene Elizabeth by her tres Patente vnder the greate seale of England beareing <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-317" data-page="317" data-folio="225" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n316_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 225">[p. 225]</button> date at Westminster the one &amp; thirteth daie of Decemb̃ in the three and forteth yeare of her reigne vpon petic̃on made vnto her by diuʳs her loueing subiectę for her royall Assent and lycense to be granted vnto them. That they at their owne Adventure Costę and chardges aswell for the honoʳ of this Realme of England as for the increase of Nauigac̃on and Advancemᵗ of trade of mʳchandize wᵗʰin the same might Adventure and sett forth cʳteine voyage wᵗʰ a Convenient nomber of shipps &amp; Pynnaces by way of traffique &amp; mʳchandize into the East Indies in the Countries and Portę of Asia &amp; Affrica did incorporate the said petitionʳs into a Bodie politique by the name of the Gouʳnoʳ and Companie of mʳchauntę of London trading into the East Indies. To haue hould and inioy the sole benefitt of the trade &amp; traffique of the said East Indies for the space of fyfteene yeares from the birth of oʳ lord god then last past before the date of the said łres Patentę. And wheareas by the said łres Patentę lycence was granted to the said Gouʳnoʳ and Company of mʳchauntę of London trading into the East Indies to transporte out of this Realme into the said Indies in euʳy of their voyadgę during the said terme of ffyfteene yeares All such forreine Coyne of Silver, Spanish or other forreine sylver, or bullion of sylver as they should during the said terme bring or cause to be brought into this Realme of England from the partę beyond the Seas, either in the same kinde, stampe, sorte or fashion wᶜʰ it should haue when they brought it in or any other forme stampe or fashion Coyned in the mynte wᵗʰin the Tower of London soe as the whole quantities of Coyne or moneys by them to be transported in any of their said voyadgę during the said terme should not exceed the value of Thirtie Thowsand poundę in any one voyadge and soe as the some of eight<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-79-fn-003" href="#sec-79-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-79-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> Thowsand poundę at the least pcell of the said some or value 30000<sup>li</sup> to be transported as aforesaid should be first coyned wᵗʰin the said Tower of London before the tyme<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-79-fn-004" href="#sec-79-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-79-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> might be transported in any of the said voyadgę as by the said łres Patentę more at lardge appeth. domynions, rather by the transportac̃on of the natiue Comodities of the same, &amp; by bartring &amp; exchandge of them for forraine Comodities, w<sup>ch</sup> now they purpose more lardglie to attempt &amp; haue good hope to accomplish then by vsing the whole libtie granted to them by the said tres Patente for carying out of the full some of Thirtie thowsand pounde in eu<sup>r</sup>y voyadge in mann<sup>r</sup> aforesaid, And that they will content themselues in this their now intended fourth voyadge to the said Indies w<sup>th</sup> the libtie of Transporting Twentie thowsand pounde onelie in forreine Coines or forreine sylver w<sup>th</sup>out other Coyne bullion or Silver.
