daye you shall feele them before you see them. And aboute Aprill the West &amp; <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-230" data-page="230" data-folio="138" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n229_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 138">[p. 138]</button> Norwest wynde begynning to blowe, soe that you may retourne agayne for the Coaste of Indea, but thinke not to touch vpon the Coaste for yt is exceeding daungerous at that tyme of the yeare, &amp; from thence till August, &amp; therefore come not neare yt, for till august yt is fowle weather, &amp; verie thicke &amp; stormye vpon the Coaste of Indea, &amp; the wynde sett right vpon the Coaste, May, Iune &amp; Iulie, but theis wynde will carrie you to Bantam, but take not too high or Sowtherlie a course for then you will hem [out] of the way of the Westerne Monsond that should helpe you in this Navigation, but comeing to Bantam before September you cannot goe for the Molloccos for the Westerne Monsonde reacheth but to Bantam. Therefore as you see cause you may touch att Pryamon where is good pepp reasonable good cheepe &amp; agood ayre.
