If not to sell for Royalls &amp; such moneys as you shall make thereof to be putt out to the Companies pffitt, as in yoʳ good discrecõn you shall thinke most meete: And for those goode brought backe by me frome the Molloccos wᵗʰ some other in the warehowse, wᶜʰ will better sell theare then heare, as mʳ Browne and mʳ Woodnoth Can best informe you, I would not that any of them should be sould theare, but be carefullie looked vnto that the perish not for lacke of eareing<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-05-fn-003" href="#sec-05-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-05-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">3</a></sup>: And yf the Hector and Suzan should retourne agayne as I make small doubt by gode helpe but they will, you maie doe as you &amp; them that come in them shall thinke most meete: But my opinion is the greater shipp is fittest to take in the goode &amp; pceede for the Molloccos for that I doubt not <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-184" data-page="184" data-folio="92" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n183_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 92">[p. 92]</button> there will be Molloccos goode enough in yo<sup>r</sup> warehowse to lade her w<sup>th</sup> Cloues. You are to haue greate care you make noe debte but such as you may att all tymes, after the expectac̃on of o<sup>r</sup> shipps w<sup>ch</sup> will be aboute 18 monnethes hence, haue in att 2 or 3 monnethes warninge: the reason is I am pswaded they will bring such order from the Company to cleare all matters out of the Countrie wherein they beare soe greate a venture w<sup>th</sup>out proffitt And therefore aboute that tyme you may haue made sale of all the Iava wares in the howse to be ymployed in pepp or any other Comoditie w<sup>ch</sup> you thinke may rebound to the Companies most pffitt.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">AND wheareas m<sup>r</sup> Gabriell Towerson<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-05-fn-004" href="#sec-05-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-05-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> I am possessed w<sup>th</sup> a conceite of yo<sup>r</sup> discrec̃on &amp; good gou<sup>r</sup>nm<sup>te</sup> I haue made choyse of you to be Commaunder ouer all this place, but to take the Councell &amp; aduice of m<sup>r</sup> Browne,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-05-fn-005" href="#sec-05-fn-005" data-fn-target="sec-05-fn-005" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> m<sup>r</sup> Woodnoth, m<sup>r</sup> Sydall, and m<sup>r</sup> Saires<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-05-fn-006" href="#sec-05-fn-006" data-fn-target="sec-05-fn-006" role="doc-noteref">3</a></sup> in all matters that the Companie maie be the better assured of all thinge that passeth</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">AND yf yt please god to laye his hand vpon you m<sup>r</sup> Towerson &amp; take you out of this world I would haue you to giue ouer your place to m<sup>r</sup> Browne, and yf you Robert Browne should die then to m<sup>r</sup> Woodnoth, &amp; yf you George Woodnoth shall die they<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-05-fn-007" href="#sec-05-fn-007" data-fn-target="sec-05-fn-007" role="doc-noteref">4</a></sup> yt shall rest in your discrec̃on to make yo<sup>r</sup> choyse of m<sup>r</sup> Sydall &amp; m<sup>r</sup> Sayrs w<sup>ch</sup> of they 2 you thinke fittest for such a place allwayes haueing a care to leaue yo<sup>r</sup> busynes in the best &amp; playnest manner you can: alsoe I doe appointe you m<sup>r</sup> Browne to be booke keep for all matters in this place.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">AND for soe much as they last voyadge there was noe order p<sup>r</sup>scribed by the Generall to such m<sup>r</sup>chaunte as he left there for <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-185" data-page="185" data-folio="93" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n184_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 93">[p. 93]</button> the keeping of each one his pticular estate in writeing soe that thereby they seemed to neglect the same as by proofe we finde for not any of the deceased haue left anie writeinge concerninge their owne estate behinde them  Therefore I doe ordeyne that eu<sup>r</sup>y m<sup>r</sup>chaunt left heere by me doe keepe an Accompt of his owne busines for the better satisfieing of the Companie &amp;
