Sir Thomas, a scion of a family of London merchants, had been knighted by James I. in 1604, and in 1609 had made an adventurous voyage to South America under the patronage of Prince Henry. After his return from India, he was in 1621 sent ambassador to Constantinople, where he remained seven years. In 1629 he was despatched on a special mission to Sweden, and afterwards to Copenhagen and various German courts. In 1641 Charles I. sent him as his representative to the Diet of Ratisbon, and on his return made him Chancellor of the order of the Garter, and a member of the Privy Council. He died in 1644.
