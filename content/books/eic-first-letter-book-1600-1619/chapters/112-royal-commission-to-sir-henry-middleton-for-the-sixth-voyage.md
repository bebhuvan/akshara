<span id="page-448" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">A Commyssion from his Mat<sup>ie</sup> to S<sup>r</sup> Henry M<sub>y</sub> ddleton knight</aside></div><p class="prose-p">JAMES,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-112-fn-001" href="#sec-112-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-112-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> by the grace of God, king of <em>England</em>, <em>Fraunce</em> and <em>Ireland</em>, defend<sup>r</sup> of the faith &amp;c. TO our trustye and welbeloued seru<sup>a</sup>nt <em>Henry Myddleton</em> knight, Greting. WHERAS, dyuers of o<sup>r</sup> louing subiecte, by the name of the <em>Gouerno<sup>r</sup> and Companye of Marchants of London trading into the East Indyes</em>, at ther owne aduentures, coste and charge, for the hono<sup>r</sup> of this o<sup>r</sup> Realme of <em>England</em>, and for the encrease and aduauncement of trade, and m<sup>r</sup>chandize w<sup>th</sup>in the same, haue set forth sundrie voyage to the <em>East Indies</em>, and alreadye discouered and begunne to settle a trade into those parte, and intending the contynuinge therof, aswell by frequenting those place alreadye setled, as by furth<sup>r</sup> discouerye of other parte, w<sup>ch</sup> may be fitt for the enterchaunging of the Comōdityes of our kingdomes, w<sup>th</sup> the Comōdityes of those place, such as may be of spīall vse and benefitte to vs and o<sup>r</sup> subiecte, hauing to this purpose now p<sup>r</sup>pared and sett forth thre Shippes to the said <em>East Indyes</em>, and the place adioyning therunto AND WHERAS our said Subiecte the Aduenturer<sup>s</sup> of those voyage, haue chosen y<sup>u</sup>, our said seru<sup>a</sup>nt <em>Henry Myddleton</em> knight, to be y<sup>e</sup> principall Gouerno<sup>r</sup> or Generall of all the m<sup>r</sup>chante, maryners and oth<sup>r</sup> our Subiecte, w<sup>ch</sup> are or shalbe shipped in the said thre Shippes, WE graciouslye fauouring the<sup>r</sup> intended voyage, and approuinge and allowinge of <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-449" data-page="449" data-folio="357" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n448_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 357">[p. 357]</button> ther choice of y<sup>u</sup> to the same gouernm<sup>t</sup>, being wylling to furnishe y<sup>u</sup> w<sup>th</sup> all fitt and conuenyent power and aucthoritye, to rule &amp; gouerne all and euerye our Subiecte, employed in this voyage, by a due obedyence to be by them yeilded vnto yo<sup>u</sup> in the obseruinge and executing of all such good orders, and constitucõns, as y<sup>u</sup> shall thinke conuenyent to ordeine and make for the furtheraunce of y<sup>e</sup> said voiage according to such pticler direccõns and enstruccõns, as y<sup>u</sup> shall receiue from y<sup>e</sup> said Gouerno<sup>r</sup> and Companye of m<sup>r</sup>chante, the Aduenturers in this voyage, Straightlye charging and comãunding all and euerye pson or psons employed in this voyage, and shipped in the said thre Shippes, to giue all due obedyence and respect vnto y<sup>u</sup> during the said voyage, and to beare themselues one towarde anoth<sup>r</sup> in good ord<sup>r</sup> and quyetnes for auoyding any occasion that might breede mutinye, quarrells or dissencoñ amongst them, to the hinderaunce of the good successe, w<sup>ch</sup> is to be hoped for by Gode prouidence of this said voyage. AND in default of such duitie and obedyence to be pfourmed towarde y<sup>u</sup>, for the correcting and quenching of all such mutinyes, quarrells or dissencons̃ that may arise, by the euyll and disorderlye disposicoñ of any pson whatsoeuer, WE do herbye aucthorize y<sup>u</sup> our said seruãnt <em>Henry Middleton</em> knight during the said voyage, or during so long tyme as y<sup>u</sup> shall liue in the said voyage, to chastice correct and punishe all Offendo<sup>rs</sup> and transgressors in that behalf according to the qualitye of ther offences, w<sup>th</sup> such punishm<sup>te</sup> as are com̃onlye vsed in all Armyes at Sea, when they are not Capitall. AND for Capitall offence as wilfull murth<sup>r</sup>, w<sup>ch</sup> is hatefull in the sight of god, or mutinye w<sup>ch</sup> is an offence w<sup>ch</sup> may tend to the ouerthrowe of the said voyage, the same being dulye and iustlye proued against any of the pson or psons aforesaid. WE do herby giue vnto y<sup>u</sup> o<sup>r</sup> said seruãnt <em>Henry Myddleton</em> knight, during all the tyme of the said voyage, or during so long tyme as y<sup>u</sup> shall liue in the said voyage, full power and aucthoritye to vse and put in execuc̃on our lawe, called Lawe Marciall in that behalf, And these our łres shalbe vnto y<sup>u</sup> sufficyent warrant and discharge, for the doing and executing of all and singłer the p<sup>r</sup>misses. AND forasmuch as at this p<sup>r</sup>sent tyme, we are in amitye w<sup>th</sup> all Christian Princes, and are vnwylling that any o<sup>r</sup> Subiecte should giue occasion of breach or <button AND forasmuch as o<sup>r</sup> said Subiectę the m<sup>r</sup>chantę and oth<sup>r</sup> the Aduenturer<sup>s</sup> of this voyadge, out of prouydence and foresight yf any mortalitye eith<sup>r</sup> by sicknes or otherwise (w<sup>ch</sup> God forbyd) should befall vnto o<sup>r</sup> seru<sup>a</sup>nt <em>Henry Myddleton</em> knight, haue made choise of o<sup>r</sup> trustie and louing subiecte Cap<sup>n</sup><sup>e</sup> <em>Nichās Dounton</em>, to succeede the said <em>Henry Myddleton</em> knight, in the charge and com̄aund of principall Gouerno<sup>r</sup> or Gen<sup>r</sup>all of all the ꝑsons employed in this voyage as aforesaid WE do alsoe (in case such an accydent should happen) allowe and approue of ther choyce in that behalf, And do herby giue full power and aucthoritye to the said Cap<sup>n</sup><sup>e</sup> <em>Nichās Dounton</em>, to do and execute all and euerye thing, incydent to the charge of principall Gouerno<sup>r</sup> or Generall of all the ꝑsons employed in this voyage as aforesaid in as ample manner and forme, and to all intentę and purposes, as is before com̄ytted and imposed vppō our Seru<sup>a</sup>nt <em>Henrye Mydleton</em> knight, And these o<sup>r</sup> łres shalbe also sufficyent warrant and discharge to the said Cap<sup>n</sup><sup>e</sup> <em>Nichās Dounton</em>, for the doing and executing of all and singuler the p<sup>r</sup>misses as afores<sup>d</sup>.
