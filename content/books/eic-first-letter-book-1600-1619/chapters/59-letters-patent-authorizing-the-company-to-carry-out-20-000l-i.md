Wheareas o<sup>r</sup> late deare sister Q: Elizabeth by her łres Patente vnder the greate seale of England beareing date at Westminster the xxxj<sup>th</sup> day of Decemb in the xliij<sup>th</sup> yeare of her Reigne vpon peticõn made vnto her by diu<sup>r</sup>s her loueing Subiectę, for her Royall assent and lycence to be graunted vnto them. To haue hould and enioye the sole benefitt of the trade and traffique of the said East Indies for the space of xv yeares from the byrth of o<sup>r</sup> Lord God then last past before the date of the said łres Patente.
