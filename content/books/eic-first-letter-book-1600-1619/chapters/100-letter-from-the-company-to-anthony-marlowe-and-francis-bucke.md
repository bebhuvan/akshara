ffrancis Bucke or either of them at Lisborne<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-100-fn-001" href="#sec-100-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-100-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup></h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">one of this Tenor deliured to m<sup>r</sup> Eldred</aside></div><p class="prose-p">AFTER o<sup>r</sup> hartie Comendac̃ons &amp;c: yo<sup>r</sup> łres dated in Lisborne the 15<sup>th</sup> of Iulie last we haue receiued whereby as we are verrie sorrie to heare of yo<sup>r</sup> hard mishapps, soe we haue done &amp; will vse all possible and speedie meanes that in vs lieth for yo<sup>r</sup> releefe and inlardgm<sup>t</sup>, &amp; will be readie to pforme what other thinge yo<sup>u</sup> doe requier of vs by yo<sup>r</sup> said łres: In the meane season we haue thought good by this occasion not onelie to make knowne vnto yo<sup>u</sup> o<sup>r</sup> greefe for this yo<sup>r</sup> distresse, but alsoe to advertice yo<sup>u</sup> that for yo<sup>r</sup> better releefe &amp; Comfort we haue dealt w<sup>th</sup> o<sup>r</sup> good freind m<sup>r</sup> Iohn Eldred,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-100-fn-002" href="#sec-100-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-100-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> to giue order to his servant W<sup>m</sup>: Squier remayneinge at Lisborne, to furnish yo<sup>u</sup> for yo<sup>r</sup> present necessities w<sup>th</sup> the some of Twoe hundred Duckette yf yo<sup>u</sup> shall need soe much &amp; further (yf need be) w<sup>th</sup> the some of three or fower hundred Duckette more, yf for the same yo<sup>u</sup> cann assuredlie by any good meanes there, worke yo<sup>r</sup> inlardgm<sup>t</sup>: Wherein yo<sup>u</sup> are to be well aduiced &amp; be of some c<sup>r</sup>teine grownde before yo<sup>u</sup> parte w<sup>th</sup> any money &amp; thus wishinge yo<sup>u</sup> to Comforte yo<sup>r</sup> selues in the lord whoe will bringe theis yo<sup>r</sup> troubles to a blessed yssewe in his good tyme as may make most to his glorie &amp; yo<sup>r</sup> Comforte in hast we end as we begunn the 14th of August 1609.
