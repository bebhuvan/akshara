<span id="page-202" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 class="sec-h2">THE IUDGE<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-40-fn-001" href="#sec-40-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-40-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> of the Admiralties warrant to app<sup>r</sup>hend such of the shipps Company as shall absent themselues.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">WHEARAS diuers of the Companie hired for the East India voyadge absent them selues from the shipps, &amp; some of them endevo<sup>r</sup> to shifte themselues away to the hindraunce of the voyadge now readie to sett sayle &amp; the m<sup>r</sup>chauntę interessed therein. Theis are to will &amp; requier you that you apprehend or cause to be app<sup>r</sup>hended all &amp; eu<sup>r</sup>y such disordered p̄sons, as shall any way Offend conc<sup>r</sup>ning the p<sup>r</sup>misses, that he or they may be deliu<sup>r</sup>ed on shippbourde to their Comaunders theare to remayne, &amp; to be p̄ceeded w<sup>th</sup> according to the quallitie of his or their offence. Whereof fayle you not as you will aunsweare the Contrarie att yo<sup>r</sup> pill. ffrom my howse neere the Doctor<sup>s</sup> Commons the 16<sup>th</sup> of March, 1606.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Your loueing freind <em>Tho Crompton.</em></p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">To m<sup>r</sup> Will̄m Pope Marshall of the Admiraltie or in his absence to the Generall of the East India Companies shipps, &amp; all other Officers of the Admiraltie, &amp; other his ma<sup>ties</sup> subiectę</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-40-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-40-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">“Whearas we haue hired diuers sailor<sup>s</sup> to goe in o<sup>r</sup> voyadge now intended for “the East Indies, whoe haue not onelie subscribed to o<sup>r</sup> Bookes, to performe the “same, but alsoe haue receiued wages of vs, &amp; some ymprest moneys: &amp; yet we “may doubt (as in former tyme hath beene vsed) that aswell before the shipps were “readie to departe as att their departure some of such a nomber may be missinge not “onelie w<sup>th</sup> o<sup>r</sup> losse, but alsoe tendinge to the ou<sup>r</sup>throwe of the wholle voyadge. Yt “is thought ffitt for prevencon hereof, That Warrant may be procured from the Judge “of the Admiraltie, or yf need be from the Lo: Admirall for app<sup>r</sup>hencon and “ponnishm<sup>t</sup> of such as are or shall goe away or departe &amp; absent them selues from “their places and service in this pnte voyadge after they shalbe hired &amp; agreed “w<sup>th</sup>all.” (<em>Court Minutes</em>, 23rd Jan., 1606/7.)
