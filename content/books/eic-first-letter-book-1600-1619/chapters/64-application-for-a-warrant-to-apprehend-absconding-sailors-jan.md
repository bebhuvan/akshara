some ymprest moneys, and yet we may doubt (as in form<sup>r</sup> tyme hath beene vsed) that aswell before the shipps were readie to depte, as at their depture some of such a nomber may be missing not onelie w<sup>th</sup> o<sup>r</sup> losse, but alsoe tending to the ou<sup>r</sup>throwe of the wholle voyadge : ffor prevencoñ thereof we humblie pray that a warrant may be graunted, or some convenient meanes vsed for app<sup>r</sup>hencõn &amp; We humblie pray that yo<sup>r</sup> Lo<sup>p</sup> : wilbe pleased to graunte yo<sup>r</sup> warrant that o<sup>r</sup> men may not be taken from vs in this tyme of necessitie : &amp; soe much the rather as the omitting of this oportunitie of bringing the said shipp now into the Docke may be the ou<sup>r</sup>throw of o<sup>r</sup> said intended voyadge.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-64-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-64-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">No date or superscription is given, but from the almost literal agreement of the wording with the resolution recorded in the Court Minutes of 23rd Jan. 1606/7 (see page 110, note), it seems probable that the present document is a copy of the application that was then ordered to be made to the Judge of the Admiralty, or, if necessary, to the Lord High Admiral.</span></li></ol></section>
