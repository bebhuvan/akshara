The w<sup>ch</sup> manner of entrie, the ffarmo<sup>rs</sup> (seekinge their owne priuate gaine haueing noe reguard to the quallitie of the same, or of o<sup>r</sup> longe &amp; Chargeable voyadge &amp; Adventure) refuce to take Vrdginge vs to enter them all for Cloues w<sup>th</sup> o<sup>r</sup> intollerable iniurie. wherefore o<sup>r</sup> humble sute is. That by yo<sup>r</sup> lo<sup>ps</sup> good meanes, this difference betweene us &amp; the ffarmo<sup>rs</sup> may be soe mediated, as that neither we, nor they may be iniured herein, &amp; that we by noe such offers of hard measure may be any way discouraged from that trade soe longe, soe dangerous &amp; chardgeable vnto vs, &amp; beneficiall to this kingdome.
