<span id="page-360" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 class="sec-h3">The Twenteth of Aprill 1608.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">A remembraunce for m<sup>r</sup> Robert Earle to be sent to Hull about veiwing of the shipp there to be sould called the m<sup>r</sup>chant Royall.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-89-fn-001" href="#sec-89-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-89-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup></p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">INPRIMIS you are w<sup>th</sup>all convenient expedi<span class="sc">c</span>õn to goe for Kingston vpon Hull or to Beverley or theareaboute to m<sup>r</sup> Humfrey Smyth of London m<sup>r</sup>chaunt at what place you may there first finde him &amp; deliu<sup>r</sup>ing him theis o<sup>r</sup> łres you are to take and ffollowe what direc<span class="sc">c</span>õns he shall giue you in this busines.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Item yo<sup>u</sup> comeinge thither and findinge (w<sup>th</sup> the advice of the said m<sup>r</sup> Smyth) that the said shipp may be for o<sup>r</sup> seruice, you are to make diligent search and veiwe of her, of what strength she is of, what crosse beames, futtick Riders,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-89-fn-002" href="#sec-89-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-89-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> bottume riders &amp; needfull stannders<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-89-fn-003" href="#sec-89-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-89-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">3</a></sup> &amp;c : she hath in her : whereby yt may be the better conceiued what the cost will be to make her fitt for o<sup>r</sup> voyadge.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Item you are c<sup>r</sup>teinelie to learne her burthen, her breadth her length : how much water she draweth in her ballast, &amp; howe much full laden, yf she be any way weakened by the longe lyinge not ymployed : her adge, &amp; yf all her tymbers be sounde.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Item you are to vnderstand &amp; see howe she is fitted w<sup>th</sup> good maste, sailes, yarde, Cables &amp; other furniture.</p></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-361"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="361" data-folio="269" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n360_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 269">[p. 269]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Item you are to make inquirie howe she is Condicioned in her saileing &amp; steridge as otherwise : &amp; alsoe what ordinance she hath, of weight and length &amp;c : &amp; of all other thinge needfull.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Item to learne her lowest price, &amp; in that price what ordinance maste sailes yarde Cables &amp; other thinge are to be sould w<sup>th</sup> her and being bought howe she may be brought hither &amp; at whose chardge &amp; what good securitie may be had for enioyinge of her, &amp; by whome yt will be geven.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Lastlie you are to make an Inventorie of all the premisses in writeinge the better to informe vs at yo<sup>r</sup> retourne, in what state she is, what she hath &amp; what she wanteth &amp; the same being pformed you are to retourne by the first meanes you may : But yf before yo<sup>r</sup> comeing thither yt shall fall out that m<sup>r</sup> Humfrey Smyth shall be retourned thence for London then you are to followe theis yo<sup>r</sup> direccons w<sup>th</sup>out him wherein you are to vse all care &amp;
