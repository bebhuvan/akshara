Threr complayning y<sup>e</sup> wrongs sustayned in y<sup>e</sup> E. Indyes by y<sup>e</sup> Hollanders.</aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">Sheweth</span>, That yo<sup>r</sup> supp<sup>t</sup>ę, hauing long and patientlye endured, sundry notorious wrongę &amp; iniuryous Courses at the handę of the <em>Hollanders</em> (in ther trade to the <em>East Indyes</em>) are enforced at last to breake sylence, and complayne their greifes; That the <em>Hollanders</em> hauing gottē into ther possessiō, dyuers of the cheif place of traffique in those p̄tę, and forciblye appropriating some, w<sup>ch</sup> do of right belong vnto the <em>English</em>, to trade ther before any oth<sup>r</sup> Natiō whatsoeu<sup>r</sup>, next vnto the <em>Portugall</em>, do seeke wholye to debarre yo<sup>r</sup> said supp<sup>t</sup>e, from the said trade, or to drawe sundrye inconuenience vppō them, no way hon<sup>ble</sup> nor p̄fitable for this Land. ffor S<sup>r</sup> ffrancys Drake, (vppō a contract w<sup>th</sup> the king of <em>Teronata</em>, for the trade of the Cloues in y<sup>t</sup> Island, and the rest of the Islandę vnder his dominiō) assisted him in his warres, against the king of <em>Tedore</em>, and confirm'd that agrem<sup>t</sup>, by delyu<sup>r</sup>ing him a Ring,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-117-fn-002" href="#sec-117-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-117-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> w<sup>th</sup> a faire <em>Emerald</em> stone in y<sup>e</sup> same, (as is well knowne to Yo<sup>r</sup> Lo<sup>pp</sup>.). M<sup>r</sup> Cauendishe afterwardę folowing him, had y<sup>e</sup> like promise at <em>Balemboa</em>, in the south p̄t of the Island of <em>Iaua</em>, wher he refreshed, conferd w<sup>th</sup> the king or cheif in that place, and had promise of all the pepp ther. Vppō w<sup>ch</sup> assurauncę, the m<sup>r</sup>chantę of Londō, and others, sett out two shippes in trade for that place, vnder the com̄aund of Cap<sup>ne</sup> <em>Rayman</em> and Cap<sup>ne</sup> <em>Lancaster;</em> In w<sup>ch</sup> accōn <em>Rayman</em> was cast away about the Cape <em>Bonæ spei</em>, and <em>Lancaster</em> p̄fourm'd his voyage, beating along the Coast of <em>India</em>, vntill he came to <em>Sumatra</em>, wher his men falling sicke, and victualls scanting, durst not aduenture for y<sup>e</sup> <em>Moluccaes</em>, but retournd w<sup>th</sup> his lading of pepp, and was wrackt in the <em>West Indyes</em>.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-117-fn-003" href="#sec-117-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-117-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">3</a></sup></p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">After them folowed the <em>Hollanders</em> w<sup>th</sup> <em>English</em> pylottę, who p̄fourming a succesfull voiage to <em>Bantā</em> haue euer since p̄secuted a most ample trade. And p̄sentlye vppō ther retourne, yo<sup>r</sup> <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-522" data-page="522" data-folio="430" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n521_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 430">[p. 430]</button> supp<sup>te</sup> as well to weaken the <em>Spanyard</em>, (then the com̄on enemye) as otherwise, ioyned in a Comp<sup>ie</sup>, and man'd forth, 4 of the best m<sup>r</sup>chant shippes of the kingdome, the <em>Dragon</em>, <em>Hector</em>, <em>Susan</em> and <em>Ascention</em>, w<sup>ch</sup> went to <em>Sumatra</em> and <em>Iaua</em>, and finding peacable trade, setled a factorye at <em>Bantā</em>, wher ther men being weake, the Cap<sup>nes</sup> thought fitt not to attempt the <em>Moluccaes</em>, (the Monsoune being then past) but hauing fought w<sup>th</sup> the Enemye in the <em>Straicte of Sincapura</em>, and takē a Carrycke, they laded ther shippes w<sup>th</sup> spice, and retournd well home, all full laden, (the <em>Hollander</em> then being very glad of yo<sup>r</sup> supp<sup>te</sup> companye.) At the retourne of w<sup>ch</sup> shippes, yo<sup>r</sup> supp<sup>te</sup> furnisht them forth againe, (vnder the com̄aund of <em>S<sup>r</sup> Henry Middletō</em> Generall) who according to his Com̄ission, attempted the <em>Moluccaes</em>, and com̄yng theth<sup>r</sup>, saued the king of <em>Teranataes</em> life, w<sup>th</sup> 6 <em>Hollanders</em>, w<sup>ch</sup> were in daung<sup>r</sup> and had bene taken by the king of <em>Tedore</em>, w<sup>th</sup> 80 men more, and cut in peece, (if he had not bene) as the rest of ther companye were, for which fauo<sup>r</sup> both the king and <em>Hollanders</em>, promised to lade ther shippe w<sup>th</sup> Cloues, and to do them any other kindnes that possiblye they could. But vppō newes of the com̄yng of certaine <em>Holland</em> shippes, they neith<sup>r</sup> pmitted them trade, according to promise, nor to staie ther, but were absolutelye banisht the Island by the <em>Hollanders</em> meanes, (as the king himself tould y<sup>e</sup> <em>English</em> the <em>Hollander</em> threatning, that if he did not, they would ioyne w<sup>th</sup> the king of <em>Tedore</em> his enemye, to driue him out of his Countrye.) <em>S<sup>r</sup> Henry</em> thervppō was enforced to pcure his whole lading from the <em>Portugall</em>, who vsed him very kindly &amp; respectiuelye, giuing him all the Cloues they had at reasonable prices; And resigned vpp vnto him, a Towne, called <em>Taffasole</em>, w<sup>th</sup> a ffort, to the king of <em>Englands</em> vse (by y<sup>e</sup> consent of the king of <em>Tedore</em> right owner therof) w<sup>ch</sup> now the <em>Hollanders</em> kepe, (hauing fortifyed the same) besides many oth<sup>r</sup> place in the Countrye; appropriating to themselues the whole trade of the Islande; keping Men of warre vppō the Coast, and phibyting all oth<sup>r</sup> Nations (as farre as in them lyeth) from any trade ther (maintayning now for Lawe, by strong hand, that w<sup>ch</sup> latelye they estemed vnreasonable: for the <em>Portugall</em> to challenge a more spīall ppertie in any of those pte, then themselues or any oth<sup>r</sup> Nation whatsoeu<sup>r</sup>.) And from thence haue pceded to the <em>Islande of Banda</em>, (the place wher Nutmege and Mace do growe,) wher at ther com̄yng w<sup>th</sup> 14 sayle of <button type="button" class="folio-trigger furth<sup>r</sup> trade, nor to gather in his debtę but w<sup>th</sup> a pemptory com̄aund, to be gone, wherunto (thorough necessitye) he submytting himself, was enforced to dept, w<sup>th</sup>out his lading, or any furth<sup>r</sup> trade.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Since that tyme, Cap<sup>ne</sup> <em>Dauyd Middletō</em> in the <em>Expedicō</em>, seking trade at
