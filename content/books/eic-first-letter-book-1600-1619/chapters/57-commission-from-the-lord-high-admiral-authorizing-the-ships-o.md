Whereas o<sup>r</sup> souereigne Lady <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-284" data-page="284" data-folio="192" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n283_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 192">[p. 192]</button> Elizabeth by the grace of god Queene of England ffraunce &amp; Ireland defendo<sup>r</sup> of the faith &amp;c : beinge Crediblie adverticed that the kinge of Spaine hath of late made staye of the shipps goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes of hir ma<sup>ties</sup> : loueing subiecte w<sup>th</sup>in the Realme of Spaine &amp; Portingale &amp; other places of his Dominions, &amp; hath caused them their<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-57-fn-004" href="#sec-57-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-57-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> being for the trade of m<sup>r</sup>chandizes to be reteyned &amp; kept in prizon Contrary to the order of Lawe &amp; Iustice.   Upon the humble and pitifull sute of hir ma<sup>ties</sup> said subiecte the m<sup>r</sup>chaunte owners &amp; others interessed in the said ships goode and psonns soe stayed made to hir ma<sup>tie</sup> for łres of Reprisalls to be graunted vnto them and that they not haueing any other meanes by order of Iustice to be yealded vnto them might by lycenced to stay apprehend and take the shipps goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes of the kinge of Spaine, or any his Subiecte wheresoeu<sup>r</sup> vpon the Seas to aunsweare and satisfie the losse and damadge by them susteyned by reason of their ships and goode soe taken and reteyned hath by her ma<sup>ties</sup> : łres Patente beareing date the ninth day of Iulie— 1585. vnder her ma<sup>ties</sup> : seale willed and authorized me as hir ma<sup>te</sup> Admirall to graunt Comission for the apprehending and takeing the shipps goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes of the kinge of Spaine, or any his subiecte to such hir ma<sup>tes</sup> loueing subiecte soe dampnified &amp; in such manner and forme and according to such articles &amp; orders as by the lorde &amp; others of her ma<sup>te</sup> privie Councell or by any six of them should be agreed vpon &amp; set downe vnder their hande.   And whereas tenn of the Lorde &amp; others of her ma<sup>ties</sup> : privy Councell haue agreed upon Certeine articles &amp; orders in that behalfe &amp; haue set downe the same in writeing vnder their hande &amp; haue deliuered the same to me for my direcc̃on herein w<sup>ch</sup> ar<sup>cles</sup> &amp; orders be &amp; remaine w<sup>th</sup> me of recorde.   And whereas further the Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and Assistante &amp; Companie of the m<sup>r</sup>chaunte of London trading for the East Indies, haue made proofe before me that certeine of their goode shipps &amp; furnished to the Seas one Shipp Called the Suzan<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-57-fn-005" href="#sec-57-fn-005" data-fn-target="sec-57-fn-005" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> of London of the burthen of 250 Tonnes or thereaboutes whereof Iohn Havard goeth Capteine &amp; Samuell Spencer m<sup>r</sup> w<sup>th</sup> mariners &amp; menn of Warr being victualled for monnethes &amp; furnished w<sup>th</sup> Caste peeces &amp; haue likewyse entred into bond in hir ma<sup>ties</sup> high Courte of the Admiralty that neither the said shipp nor any the Company thereof shall not attempt against any hir ma<sup>ties</sup> loueing subiecte the subiecte of the ffrentch kinge the kinge of Scotland Denmarke or Sweden or any the Inhabitants of the United provinces of the Haunce Townes or lowe Countries or the subiecte of any other Prince or potentate being in good league and amity w<sup>th</sup> her ma<sup>tie</sup>, but onelie against the Subiecte of the kinge of Spaine: And alsoe shall bring or cause to be brought all such shipps goode or m<sup>r</sup>chandizes as they shall soe take &amp; apprehend of the kinge of Spaine or any his subiecte together w<sup>th</sup> three or fower of the Company of eu<sup>r</sup>y such prize as shall be of most accompte whereof the m<sup>r</sup> &amp; Pilott to be twoe to some porte of this hir ma<sup>ties</sup> Realme of England as shall be most Convenient for them, And shall not breake, bulke, waste, spoyle, sell or diminish any such shipps &amp; goode soe taken vntill the shall be adiudged in hir ma<sup>ties</sup> high Courte of the Admiraltie to be lawfull prize. And therevpon an Inventorie to be taken by the viceadmirall of the place or his Deputie &amp; other publique Officers of the same, And appraisment made of the same goode by some six honest menn Inhabitante of the said Porte where they shall soe arriue, and that the same Inventory &amp; appraism<sup>t</sup> shall be retourned into hir ma<sup>ties</sup> high Courte of the Admiraltie w<sup>th</sup>in six weeke then next ensueing. Then the said ships goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes to keepe and reteyne in his or their possession &amp; to make sale &amp; dispose thereof in open m<sup>r</sup>kett or howsoeur els to their best benefitt in as ample &amp; beneficiall manner, as if the said ships goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes had bene Come by, through the lawfull traffique &amp; trade of m<sup>r</sup>chaunte or of iust prize in the tyme of open warr betwixt hir ma<sup>tie</sup> &amp; the said kinge of Spaine soe that neither the said Capteine master nor any the Company thereof that shall in his or their owne psonn serue in the shipp aforesaid, or otherwyse bear Chardge in the Adventure, or in any other sorte further or sett forewarde the said enterprize in manner &amp; forme aforesaid, shall not be reputed or challendged for any offendo<sup>r</sup> against hir ma<sup>ties</sup> lawes and that alsoe it shall be lawfull for any manner of pson to buye the said ships goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandizes soe as aforesaid taken prayzed Inventoryed &amp; adiudged w<sup>th</sup>out any daunger losse hindraunce trouble molestac̃on or incumbraunce whatsoeur Provided alwaies that yf by Colo<sup>r</sup> of this Comission there shall be taken any shipps goode or m<sup>r</sup>chandizes of any hir ma<sup>ties</sup>: loueing subiecte the subiecte of the ffrench kinge, the kinge of Scotland Denmarke or Sweden or any the Inhabitante of the united prouinces of the hance Townes or lowe Countries or the subiecte of any other Prince or Potentate being in good league &amp; amity w<sup>th</sup> hir ma<sup>tie</sup> &amp; the same or any other shipps taken by authoritie hereof shalbe spoyled sould imbeaselled or dyminished before they shall be adiudged in her ma<sup>ties</sup> high Courte of the Ad<sup>ty</sup>: to belong to the king of Spaine, or any of his subiecte that then this Comission to be void and of noe sufficient authoritie, either to take the said shipps &amp; the shipps of warr shall be Confiscate to o<sup>r</sup> vse. Prouided alsoe that the said shipps goode and m<sup>r</sup>chandizes soe as is aforesaid, after an Inventory taken, appraism<sup>t</sup> made &amp; iudgment geven in manner and forme aforesaid shall be equallie devided into three p<sup>te</sup>, whereof one p<sup>te</sup> to goe to the m<sup>r</sup>chaunt<em>e</em> &amp; owners of the said shipp, another p<sup>te</sup> to the victuallers &amp; the third parte to the Captaine master mariners &amp; souldiers thereof.
