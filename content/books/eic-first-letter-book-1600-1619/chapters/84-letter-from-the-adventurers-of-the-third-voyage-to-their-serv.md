the Vnion of w<sup>ch</sup> shipps and men by Comission from o<sup>r</sup> kinge ma<sup>tie</sup> : m<sup>r</sup> Alexander Sharpie is o<sup>r</sup> Generall in the Assencõn &amp; m<sup>r</sup> Richard Rowles his Leiuetenante Gen<sup>r</sup>all in the Vnion by whome we haue now sent theis o<sup>r</sup> lres vnto you, and thoughe wee cannot but giue you to vnderstand that this voyadge is solie one ioincte stocke seu<sup>r</sup>all from any of the former voyadge, yett the same is sett forth by some alsoe interessed in the third voyadge, whoe are authorized &amp; allowed by the gen<sup>r</sup>all Companie to doe the same And are inclined &amp; authorized the next yeare to doe the like. Wherefore as the forenamed Generall and others now sent haue order for their pte to doe the best Assistaunce they cann, soe we wish you to shew them all kindnes and favo<sup>r</sup> you may, aswell in the direccõn of their trade &amp; saffetie as otherwise, yett soe as the Accompte be noe way mingled, nor the proceedinge of either, be any way preiudiceall thone to thother &amp; they Comeing to any place wherein you shall be Resident as loueing Brothers and servante of one Companie you are to instruct them by the best meanes you may howe you haue there proceeded w<sup>th</sup> the Superio<sup>rs</sup> of the place, &amp; what you haue done conc<sup>r</sup>ninge the setlinge of yo<sup>r</sup> trade, &amp; howe the same is likelie to stand. And further theis are to requier you that if the shipps of the Adventurers in the 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge shall happen to come where you shall be resident &amp; wante ladeing of their owne. That then &amp; in such case you haueing any goodę to send home you doe lade soe much of the best thereof as convenientlie they shall be able to take in, being it much better that the same be soe brought home then that it should lie theare dead : wherein you are to haue due care that the Accomptę of either pte be noe way mingled &amp; that yo<sup>r</sup> goodę sent doe come vnder a seu<sup>r</sup>all Marke, Consigned by Bills of ladinge to the vse of the Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and Companie of the Adventurers in the third voyadge, takeing the best order that you can for keepinge of the seu<sup>r</sup>all goodę of both voyadgę apte thone from thother &amp; that łres be writte what shall be remaineinge for each Accompt. And further you shall vnderstand That if by theis meanes you shall send home any of o<sup>r</sup> goodę by the said shipps of the Adventurers in the 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge &amp; for the same or for some other cause there shall be noe need or ymployment for soe many of o<sup>r</sup> ffactor<sup>s</sup> there w<sup>ch</sup> may happen to be left.
