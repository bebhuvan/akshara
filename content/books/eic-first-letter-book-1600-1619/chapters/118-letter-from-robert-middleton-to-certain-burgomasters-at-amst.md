Vppō some speeches had at my being at <em>Amsterdam</em>, in opening the wronge, w<sup>ch</sup> I conceyued the <em>East India Comp<sup>ie</sup> of London</em> had sustayned by yo<sup>r</sup> people, in those pte, I found yo<sup>r</sup> inclynatiō and readynes to ioyne in conference by some Com̄issioners to be nōīated for eith<sup>r</sup> pt. Wheruppō at my retourne, into <em>England</em>, I acquainted o<sup>r</sup> Gouern<sup>r</sup>, Depuitye and Com̄yttees w<sup>th</sup> that motiō. But before my com̄yng, they had exhibyted a petic̃on to my Lo. Thrēr,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-118-fn-002" href="#sec-118-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-118-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup> to acquaint the State w<sup>th</sup> ther wronge, and to craue redresse for the same. Notw<sup>th</sup>standing vppō my relac̃on, I found them wylling to staie any furth<sup>r</sup> pceding, as yet, but to ioyne in any good course, to haue all wronge righted, and all iust cause of offence remou’d betwixt thē and y<sup>u</sup>. That as o<sup>r</sup> Nations haue <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-525" data-page="525" data-folio="433" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n524_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 433">[p. 433]</button> long contynued, in firme bonde and league of amytye, so we might peacablye pcede to trade ioynctlye togeth<sup>r</sup> (w<sup>th</sup>out troubling of eith<sup>r</sup> <em>States</em> (to right conceyued iniuryes) or giuing such an aduauntage to others (who would willinglye take the opportuntye of fishing in the troubled streames of o<sup>r</sup> diuision and dissencõns). So that (vppon conference) they are wylling to ioyne Com̄ission<sup>r</sup>s, w<sup>th</sup> such as y<sup>u</sup> shall substitute here, to salue and put a remedie to all greifes past, and consid<sup>r</sup> of some indifferent course to be contynued betwixt vs heraft<sup>r</sup>, for the good and benefitte of eith<sup>r</sup> pt. Wherin I could not retourne a more spedie resolucoñ, because of some extraordinarye occasions of launching certaine shippes, and oth<sup>r</sup> buysines of great import, w<sup>ch</sup> then hindred them; but haue now w<sup>th</sup> the first opportunitye, acquainted y<sup>u</sup> therw<sup>th</sup>, that if y<sup>u</sup> shall determyne of psecuting the form<sup>r</sup> pposicõn, and appoint some Com̄ission<sup>r</sup>s frō y<sup>u</sup>, it might be done spedilye (for the good I hope of both the Nations) and that I might receiue yo<sup>r</sup> resolucõns herin, w<sup>th</sup> all expedicõn. And so recomēnding the care herof to yo<sup>r</sup> graue consideracõns, and the successe to gode puidence, whome we beseech to direct all o<sup>r</sup> pcedinge to his glorie, (my self being diuoted in all true affeccõn to do all acceptable office I may for the good of both) I take my leaue, and rest,</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Yo<sup>rs</sup> to be com̄aunded</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Directed To the right Wo<sup>rll</sup>, my most respected friende, Burgom<sup>r</sup> Oatgens, Burgom<sup>r</sup> Pavij, Skepen Hasselare, Skepen Iacop gerson heind, Pentionalis Pavij, Dericke van Os, and Iohn van Harell, at <em>Amsterdā</em>, hast yēs.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>London, 5º Octob: 1611.</em></p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ffor aunswer of w<sup>ch</sup> łre, an ord<sup>r</sup> of Court was retournd from thence, w<sup>ch</sup> is recorded in the <em>Court booke.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-118-fn-003" href="#sec-118-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-118-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> pag. 271.</em></p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-118-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-118-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">Robert Middleton, who seems to have been related in some way to Sir Henry Middleton, was a prominent and active member of the Company from its formation, and was a “committee” for a long period.
