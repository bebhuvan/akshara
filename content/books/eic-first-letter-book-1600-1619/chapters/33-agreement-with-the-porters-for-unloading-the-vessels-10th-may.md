In Considerac̃on of w<sup>ch</sup> seu<sup>r</sup>all somes the said Porters for them &amp; eu<sup>r</sup>y of them Covenant pmyse &amp; agree to and w<sup>th</sup> the said Comitties. That they the said Porters shall att their own pp chardge slinge Craine lade in &amp; and out of Carte &amp; Carte, &amp; Carrie weighe and stowe the said Companies goode now to be vnladen out of the shipps latelie arriued, or cause the same to be done in manner as aforesaid And alsoe to satisfie the said Companie for whatsoeu<sup>r</sup> damadge shall happen to the same goode by the default or ymbecilm<sup>t</sup> of the said Porters or any other whome they shall appointe to this busines, either in Craying or while the same goode shall be vnder their power or service to be done in the premisses.
