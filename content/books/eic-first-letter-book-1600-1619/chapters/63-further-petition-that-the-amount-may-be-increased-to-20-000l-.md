We therefore humblie pray that by yo<sup>r</sup> honorable good favo<sup>rs</sup> his ma<sup>tie</sup> may be now moued to vouchsaffe vs his highnes graunte for transportac̃on in theis o<sup>r</sup> ships for the East Indies the some of 20000<sup>li</sup> in forreine Coines in place of the said 15000<sup>li</sup> w<sup>th</sup>out new coyning any pte thereof. And as most bound we pray god giue you all health &amp; happines.</p></div>
