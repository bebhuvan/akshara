m<sup>r</sup> Rowles</aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">Forasmuch</span> as since yo<sup>r</sup> depture we haue receiued noe łres from yo<sup>u</sup>, nor M<sup>r</sup> Rowles and yett haue hard by łres written from Cairo home (thoughe vncertaine intelligence) that yo<sup>u</sup> haue had good traffique at Mocho both in sellinge o<sup>r</sup> English Comodities and byinge Indico and other m<sup>r</sup>chandize whereof we hope (though we haue not receiued them) yo<sup>u</sup> haue written łres by the Carivans and sent Iohn Iourden over land according to yo<sup>r</sup> Comission, w<sup>ch</sup> yf yo<sup>u</sup> haue not done will argue greate remisnes &amp; may not onlie greatelie hinder the Company, but alsoe discouradge such ffacto<sup>rs</sup> as yo<sup>u</sup> haue lefte in those parte, for want of supplies Wherefore we haue by S<sup>r</sup> Henry Middleton Generall of this o<sup>r</sup> sixt voyadge not onlie written theis few lines vnto yo<sup>u</sup> but alsoe by him sent łres vnto any such ffactory as yo<sup>u</sup> may in any place haue planted praying yf by the prouidence of God yo<sup>u</sup> shall meete together that vpon receipt of this o<sup>r</sup> łre yo<sup>u</sup> would relate vnto S<sup>r</sup> Hen: Middleton, [or] his viceadmirall one m<sup>r</sup> ffeemell the whole proceedinge of yo<sup>r</sup> voyadge and to aduise him where yo<sup>u</sup> haue traded, where yo<sup>u</sup> finde least danger, &amp; most apparance of proffitt, As alsoe yf yo<sup>u</sup> haue lefte any of o<sup>r</sup> stocke behinde, where, and at whose hande yt may be demanded to thend he may accordinge to order giuen him send or bringe home the same when he retourneth.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ffurther we desier yo<sup>u</sup> to assist him and m<sup>r</sup> Lawrance ffeemell w<sup>th</sup> yo<sup>r</sup> best advice not onlie w<sup>ch</sup> of those ffacto<sup>rs</sup> yo<sup>r</sup> selfe haue placed in that Country, but alsoe w<sup>ch</sup> amongst all the ffacto<sup>rs</sup> that wee haue either at Aden, Suratt, Bantam, or ells where w<sup>th</sup> whome yo<sup>u</sup> may haue conversed are the fittest men to be ymployed in o<sup>r</sup> service both in reguard of their honest carriadge, and sufficient knowledge of that Country Comodities.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp;
