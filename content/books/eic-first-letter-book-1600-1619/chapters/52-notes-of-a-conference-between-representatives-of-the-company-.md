agreem<sup>t</sup> betweene the Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and others of the <em>East India Comp:</em> &amp; the ffarmo<sup>rs</sup> of his ma<sup>ties</sup> Customes in the presence of the Lo: Thrēr the 5th of Aprill. 1607.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">THAT wheareas question is made touching the validitie in lawe of the Patent of the East India Companie for that pointe of transportac̃on free of Custome, of such goodę brought in by them haueing form<sup>r</sup>lie paied Custome inwardę<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-52-fn-002" href="#sec-52-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-52-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">2</a></sup>: &amp; the matter in some sorte debated before his L<sup>p</sup>: betwixt the Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> of the said Companie &amp; the ffarmo<sup>rs</sup> of his ma<sup>te</sup> Customes, soe as his L: is pleased to write his l̃re to the kingę Atturney gen<sup>r</sup>all for his opinion touchinge the same.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Note that his Lp: is furder to be remembred according vnto his expresse pleasure that yf the said Pattent be founde insufficient in Lawe that then his Lp: is pleased to pcure a privie seale from his ma<sup>tie</sup>, that the said Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> &amp; Companie, or any free brother of the said Comp: that shall buy any of the said Companies goode, may in gen<sup>r</sup>all &amp; in pticuler transporte all or pte of the said goode brought in by them free of Custome w<sup>th</sup>in thirteene monnethes after the dischardginge of the said goode, soe as the subsidie &amp; Custome for the same haue beene paied or Compounded for inwarde. And that for such goode as haue latelie beene brought in by the said Companie for w<sup>ch</sup> the Custome is paied for &amp; Compounded, may w<sup>th</sup>in 13 monnethes after the dischardge of the said goode be freelie transported by any brother of the Companie, or any other of the kinge ma<sup>te</sup> subiecte w<sup>th</sup>out any further paym<sup>t</sup> of Subsidie, or Custome for the same. And further to expresse in the said privie seale, that yt may be lawfull for the said Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and Companie to shipp out their goode for the East Indies free of Custome: 4 voyages accordinge to the Teno<sup>r</sup> of their Charter.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-52-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-52-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">This memorandum is entered, but without explanation or comment, on the Court Minutes for 9th May, 1607.
