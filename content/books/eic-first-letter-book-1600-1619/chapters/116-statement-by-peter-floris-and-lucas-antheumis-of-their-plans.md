And if we should not sell all o<sup>r</sup> com̃odityes, by the moneth of Nouemb̃r, then we purpose to settle a factorye in <em>Patane</em> or <em>Siā</em>, w<sup>th</sup> the rest of the goodꬵ, ther to trade w<sup>th</sup> the <em>China</em> Iunckes, in March next following, and the shippe w<sup>th</sup> the rest of the aduaunced Capitall, shall in the moneth of Nouemb̃r, take her voyage, thorough the Straictꬵ of <em>Sincapura</em>, by <em>Malacca</em>, and so againe towardꬵ the Coast of <em>Coromandell</em>, and ther wyll sell o<sup>r</sup> Comodityes, as gould, mony, and oth<sup>r</sup> thinges, for Cottō lynnen whight and paynted, both to trade againe w<sup>th</sup>all, at <em>Patane</em> and <em>Siam</em>, as also to bring homewardꬵ for <em>England</em>; as also a good pcell of Cottō yarne. And then in the moneth of May A° 1613, we meane to set sayle againe from y<sup>e</sup> Coastꬵ of <em>Coromandell</em>, to the Straictꬵ of <em>Sincapura</em>, and so againe to <em>Patane</em> and <em>Syam</em>, and ther traffique vntill the moneth of Nouemb̃r, and then take o<sup>r</sup> course towardꬵ <em>Bantā</em>; and from thence in <em>December</em> 1613, or in Ianuarye 1614, take o<sup>r</sup> course towardꬵ <em>England</em>, wher we hope to arryue in <em>August</em> 1614. hauing laden into o<sup>r</sup> shippe so much China silke, Taffataes, Damaskes, and Pursalyne, as we may attayne vnto, or as o<sup>r</sup> Capitall will pmytte, and we thinke may be here vented in <em>England</em>, Also a good pcell of <em>Beniamyn</em>, a pcell of <em>Lignū Aloes</em>, a lyttle <em>Calumback</em>, <em>Muske</em> frō <em>Syam</em>, <em>Gum Lack</em>, Also 150 bales of Cottō lynnen, whight and paynted, the w<sup>ch</sup> will serue to vent in <em>Europe</em>, but espīallye for the Coast of <em>Barbarye</em>, and some for the Coast of <em>Ginnye</em>, <em>Angola</em>, and oth<sup>r</sup> place, and about 200 bales of Cottō yarne; and for a proofe a pcell of <em>Indico</em> frō the coast of <em>Coromandell</em>, and some other drugges and wares, the w<sup>ch</sup> we may light vppō, and as o<sup>r</sup> capitall shall pmitte vs to deale in;
