<span id="page-365" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">A Bill of Adventure for the 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge</aside></div><p class="prose-p">WHEREAS<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-91-fn-001" href="#sec-91-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-91-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> <strong>A B</strong> Citizen and &amp;c . . . . . . one of the Adventurers and one of the Brethren of the Companie of Marchaunt<em>e</em> of London tradinge into the East Indies hath not onelie sett downe for his Adventure w<sup>th</sup> the said Company to the some of 550<sup>li</sup> in their 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge to the East Indies by the Cape <em>Bona spei:</em> in the good shipps called the Assenc̃on &amp; the Vnion But hath alsoe trulie paied the said some of 550<sup>li</sup> for his Adventure to Richard Atkinson Casheare to the said Adventurers in the 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge. We therefore the Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and Company doe by this o<sup>r</sup> present Bill of Adventure promyse and agree to and w<sup>th</sup> the said <strong>A B.</strong> That we the said Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> and Comp : and o<sup>r</sup> Successor<sup>s</sup> shall and will vpon the retourne of the ships called the Assenc̃on &amp; Vnion or either of them sett out by vs in this present fourth voyadge or w<sup>th</sup>in Convenient tyme after their said retourne in the same deliu<sup>r</sup> vnto the said <strong>A B</strong> ..... his Execū Administ: or Assignes atrue and iust Accompt and paym<sup>t</sup> of such stocke benefitt and proffitt of stocke as god shall send vpon the said voyadge according to the gen<sup>r</sup>all distribuc̃on pporc̃on and allotm<sup>t</sup> w<sup>ch</sup> shall be allotted dispôsed and given to all &amp; eu<sup>r</sup>y the seu<sup>r</sup>all Adventurers except &amp; alwaies reserued out of the said some or stocke before mencioned for and to thuse of the right owners of the same all such somes w<sup>ch</sup> shall in due tyme iustlie appeare to</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-91-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-91-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">1.</a> <span class="fn-body">“Was red a Bill of Adventure, to be made &amp; given vnder the Companies seale “to all &amp; eu<sup>r</sup>y the principall Adventurers in the 4<sup>th</sup> voyadge to theast Indies &amp; ordered “to be engrossed &amp; deliu<sup>r</sup>ed accordinglie.” (<em>Court Minutes</em>, 22nd Nov., 1608.)</span></li></ol></section>
