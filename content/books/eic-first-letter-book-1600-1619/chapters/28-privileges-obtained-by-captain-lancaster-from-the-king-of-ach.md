<span id="page-161" class="sec-page-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Theis 3 foresaid łres were translated out of the India speech into English.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">I MOSTE<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-001" href="#sec-28-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> mightie kinge of Dachem and Sumatra to all psons that shall read this present writeinge, Greeting.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="sidenote-stack"><aside class="shoulder-sidenote">Priuiledges from the kinge of Dachem and Sumatra to the Companie.</aside></div><p class="prose-p"><span class="sc">In token</span> of o<sup>r</sup> espetiall freindshipp &amp; vpon many good Consideracõns vs moueing, and cheeflie vppon the Contemplasing of the gratious łres receiued from the most famous Queene of England: we of o<sup>r</sup> espetiall meere motion doe signifie and declare to all people, that we haue intertayned into o<sup>r</sup> freindshipp, <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-162" data-page="162" data-folio="70" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n161_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 70">[p. 70]</button> &amp; holie league o<sup>r</sup> well beloued the <em>SERINISSIMA REGINA DE ENGLA TERRA</em>. to hold and keepe true and faithfull league w<sup>th</sup> her accordinge to the Comendable Course &amp; law of all nations, vnto whose subiecte, we wish much felicitie and therefore doe giue and graunte by theis p<sup>r</sup>sente for vs o<sup>r</sup> heires &amp; successor<sup>rs</sup> as much as in vs lieth to the said subiecte of the most noble Queene of England o<sup>r</sup> Confederate &amp; eu<sup>r</sup>y of them theis articles, graunte &amp; priuiledge heereafter expressed and declared.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">FFIRSTE we doe giue and graunte ffree licence aucthoritie &amp; power to all the people, ffactor<sup>s</sup>, m<sup>r</sup>chaunte or subiecte of the Queene of England that they may att all tymes heereafter for eu<sup>r</sup> suerlie and saffelie come into any portes or els wheare into theis o<sup>r</sup> dominiones &amp; kingdome of Sumatra w<sup>th</sup> their shipps goode &amp; m<sup>r</sup>chandize w<sup>th</sup>out any deteinm<sup>te</sup> or hindraunce to them, or to their goode: &amp; theare to abide soiourne, buie &amp; sell, barter and exchaunge according to their owne<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-002" href="#sec-28-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> manner and fashion w<sup>th</sup> all manner of Nations whatsoeu<sup>r</sup> as well as w<sup>th</sup> o<sup>r</sup> owne naturall subiecte: both in spices &amp; all other m<sup>r</sup>chandizes &amp; soe they may tarrie in o<sup>r</sup> Countrie soe long as they will, &amp; goe away when the list, w<sup>th</sup>out any ympedim<sup>t</sup> lett or hindraunce, paying all such debte as they shall owe to any of o<sup>r</sup> subiecte w<sup>th</sup>in o<sup>r</sup> said dominion.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">FFURTHERMORE o<sup>r</sup> will &amp; pleasure is that all such goode and m<sup>r</sup>chandize as any of the subiecte of the Queene of England shall bringe into this o<sup>r</sup> kingdome or any the partes thereof shall be ffree of all Custome or paym<sup>t</sup> whatsoeu<sup>r</sup> as well for that w<sup>ch</sup> shall be brought into theis o<sup>r</sup> dominions as for all manner of m<sup>r</sup>chandize they shall transporte out of the same for any forreine pte whatsoeu<sup>r</sup>.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM yf any of their shipps in tempest of weather shall be in daunger to be lost, &amp; pishe, &amp; therevpon shall stande in neede of o<sup>r</sup> helpe wee will and Comaund that o<sup>r</sup> vessells w<sup>th</sup> all speede doe helpe &amp; succo<sup>r</sup> them, to saue the same shipps &amp; goode and what may be saued, to retourne to the said m<sup>r</sup>chaunte or their assignes they paying resonable Considerac̃on for their travell.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM yf any Englishe man shall make his will &amp; Testam<sup>te</sup> to <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-163" data-page="163" data-folio="71" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n162_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 71">[p. 71]</button> whomesoeu<sup>r</sup> (by the same will [ )] he shall giue his goode, the ptie shall haue them accordinglie, and yf he die intestate, he to whome the cheefe ffacto<sup>r</sup> or gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> shall saie the goode of the dead to belonge, he shall posses the same.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM that eu<sup>r</sup>y b̄gaine made by the said m<sup>r</sup>chauntę w<sup>th</sup> any manner of p̄son for any kinde of m<sup>r</sup>chandize shall be firme and staple &amp; that noe man of either ptie may shrinke or goe backe from the same &amp; yt shall be lawfull for them to goe before any of o<sup>r</sup> Iustices &amp; to register the same in a Booke according to the teno<sup>r</sup> thereof, yf any Controu<sup>r</sup>sie shall aryse present iustice to be done to both p̄ties, &amp; when they haue sold any of their wares to o<sup>r</sup> subjectę or any other they shall not retourne the said wares vpon the m<sup>r</sup>chauntę hande, but paie for them or ells they shall haue present iustice</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM yf any English m<sup>r</sup>chaunte ffacto<sup>r</sup> or servant shall offend the Agent or cheefe Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> of the ffactorie yt shall be lawfull for the said Gouerno<sup>r</sup> to doe iustice vpon the said ptie, or to send him home into England att his pleasuer, &amp; further we graunte vnto the said Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup> power and aucthoritie to end any Controu<sup>r</sup>sie that shall aryse betweene them &amp; to doe iustice accordinge to their owne lawes and Customes.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM we doe graunte the said marchauntę and to their Successo<sup>rs</sup>, that yf any of them or of their servantę shall be wounded or be slaine in any pte or place of o<sup>r</sup> dominion, then informačon thereof being geven we o<sup>r</sup> iustices and other Officers will execute due correcčon &amp; punishm<sup>t</sup> w<sup>th</sup>out delaie accordinge to the cause of the offence, soe that yt shall be an example to all others not to offend in the like. And yf it shall happen the ffactors servauntę or mynisters of the said m<sup>r</sup>chauntę to trespasse or offend, whereby they or any of them shall incurr the daunger of death, or of punishm<sup>t</sup>, that then the goode wares or m<sup>r</sup>chandizes of their Senio<sup>rs</sup> shall not therefore be forfected Confiscate seazed vpon nor spoyled by vs o<sup>r</sup> heires or Successo<sup>rs</sup> or by any of o<sup>r</sup> Officers ministers or subiectę but shall remaine franke &amp; free as dischardged of all punishm<sup>t</sup> &amp; losse.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM we doe p̄myse the foresaid m<sup>r</sup>chauntę graunting yt for eu<sup>r</sup> for vs &amp; o<sup>r</sup> heires that yf any shall bringe any manner of goode or m<sup>r</sup>chandize meete for Treasurie that we will not cause anie <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-164" data-page="164" data-folio="72" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n163_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 72">[p. 72]</button> staie or arrest to be made of their said goode or m<sup>r</sup>chaundize to vs or for o<sup>r</sup> vses w<sup>th</sup>out present Content to be made by o<sup>r</sup> Thr̄er at such reasonable prises as the m<sup>r</sup>chaunte could sell them for ready money, &amp; that noe price or valuacõn shalbe set vpon their goode or m<sup>r</sup>chandizes by vs or any Officers of o<sup>rs</sup>.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM we of o<sup>r</sup> goodnes haue graunted to the said English m<sup>r</sup>chaunte their successo<sup>rs</sup> servaunte &amp; deputies that doe or shall remayne in any pte of this o<sup>r</sup> kingdome of Sumatra whether yt be in Dachem or any other pte of o<sup>r</sup> dominion freelie to keepe their owne lawe &amp; in any wyse none of o<sup>rs</sup> to force them to o<sup>r</sup> lawe or faith against their wills.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">ITEM yf any man shall saie, that theis being Christians haue spoken any thinge to the derogacõn of o<sup>r</sup> holie faith &amp; religion &amp; haue slaundered the same, in this matter as well as in all others there shall noe false witnes in any case be admitted.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">LAST of all to thintent that this league w<sup>th</sup> her ma<sup>tie</sup> &amp; the aforesaid graunte &amp; priuiledge w<sup>th</sup> her subiecte, may the better be obserued and inuiolablie be pformed, we by the grace of God greate kinge of Dachem &amp; Sumatra for vs o<sup>r</sup> heires &amp; sucssessors doe pmyse by o<sup>r</sup> princelie word in stead of an Oath inviolablie to maintaine &amp; preserue &amp; will cause to be inuiolablie kept p<sup>r</sup>served &amp; mayntayned frome tyme to tyme &amp; att all tymes heereafter &amp; for the more ratificacõn we comaund all o<sup>r</sup> Captaines Iudges Customers Gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>rs</sup> &amp; servaunte wheresoeu<sup>r</sup> theis łres or priuiledge shall be seene throughout all o<sup>r</sup> dominions that they be obeyed in all pointe accordinglie as longe as the Queene of England of her pte shall duelie keepe and obserue this league &amp; holie peace expressed in this priuiledge w<sup>th</sup> vs and o<sup>r</sup> subiecte we alsoe of o<sup>r</sup> Royall pte doe chardge &amp; Comaund the same soe longe to be strayghtlie kept &amp; obserued.
