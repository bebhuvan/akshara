Wᶜʰ we finde must eyther stand or fall by such a moderac̃on as is to be carried herein, and fallinge into Considerac̃on of the first offer of favoʳ to the said Companie mencioned in yoʳ lᵖˢ Lres wᶜʰ was that a ioynct sale of his maᵗⁱᵉˢ pepp wᵗʰ a like quantitie of the Companies pepp should be vttered and sold to gether equallie &amp; not otherwyse.  Wee finde in the Examinãcõn of that Course theis pticuler ympedimᵗᵉ followinge first agreate quantitie of the Companies pepp alredy disposed into many hande by a genʳal diuident agreed vpon amongst them selues before yoʳ ll: łres came to oʳ hande, beside other great quantities of pepp brought out of the lowe Countries and both of them soe passed from hand to hand from the mʳchaunt to the Grocer &amp; from the Grocer to the Chapman in the Countrey that by this meanes there is sufficient pepp alreadie deliuʳed out abroade in the Cittie and Countrie, to forbeare any further sale for one yeare att the least, soe as the vent of his maᵗⁱᵉˢ pepp by a ioynt retayling sale wᵗʰ the remaynder of the Companies pepp cann noe way advaunce any some of money in Convenient tyme to his maᵗⁱᵉˢ vse, worthie the attending the Event of this course.  Wᶜʰ being soe then their resteth onelie the other meane wᶜʰ is the sale of the kinge pepp to the Companie, <button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-137" data-page="137" data-folio="45" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/registerofletter00eastuoft/page/n136_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 45">[p. 45]</button> wherein we they Comitties on the said Companies behalfe doe humblie informe yo<sup>r</sup> ℔ that the said Companie haue vpon their hande alredie soe greate a masse of pepp, that allbeyt eu<sup>r</sup>y Adventurer thereof hath free libtie for the bringing in of 100<sup>li</sup> of money to take out vC<sup>li</sup> worth of pepper vpon his stocke to set forth a new voyadge to the East Indies to fetch home the remaynder of their goode there yet in reguard there is noe present sale thereof there is such drawing backe in pformeinge of this supplie that the voyadg hath not that expedi<span class="sc">c</span>õn w<sup>ch</sup> weare Convenient. Wherevpon we thinke that soe many pticuler men of soe diuers condi<span class="sc">c</span>õn will hardlie be drawen to ingage them selues for their seu<sup>r</sup>all propor<span class="sc">c</span>õns of their adventures to inlardg anie furder somes to buie a Comoditie that is like to lie soe longe vpon their hande. Vpon the Considera<span class="sc">c</span>õn of w<sup>ch</sup> difficultie, and the vnlikeliehood that the Generallitie of the Companie will be drawen to enterteyne this bargaine, we thought yt not meete to call the Companie together to propound this mo<span class="sc">c</span>õn vnto them, least yt should not onelie receiue a deniall, but being a matter putt into soe many distasted humo<sup>rs</sup> would be hardlie receiued and spoken off. We therefore seing noe present course to be followed that would bringe forth either benefitt to his ma<sup>tie</sup> or the Ccmpany, though[t] ffitt to shewe to yo<sup>r</sup> hono<sup>rs</sup> vpon what ympedim<sup>te</sup> we rest, desyreing to be furder directed herein. And we the Comitties for the Company doe humblie adu<sup>r</sup>tise yo<sup>r</sup> ℔ that yf the prepara<span class="sc">c</span>õn of the voyadge be any way crossed by vrging the gen<sup>r</sup>alitie w<sup>th</sup> further supplies of chardge, or hindring them in the vtteringe of their pepp diuided &amp; to be devided amongst them before the deptinge of the ffleete w<sup>ch</sup> will be aboute the end of ffebruarie, the voyadge will whollie be ou<sup>r</sup>throwne w<sup>ch</sup> may by yo<sup>r</sup> Lo<sup>ps</sup> ffavo<sup>r</sup> be vphoulded both to the benefitt of the kinge and the good of the Comon wealth by this small tollera<span class="sc">c</span>õn &amp; the shipps being once sent away the Company will be redie in all dutie to submitt them selues to any Course that shall seeme beneficiall to his ma<sup>tie</sup> for the venting of his pepp. And thus humblie resting at yo<sup>r</sup> ll Comaundem<sup>t</sup> &amp; service we take our leaue London the 8<sup>th</sup> of Decemb̃ 1603.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Yo<sup>r</sup> Lo<sup>ps</sup> humble att Comaund</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">W<sup>m</sup> Rider</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">Thomas Smyth gou<sup>r</sup>no<sup>r</sup></p></div>
