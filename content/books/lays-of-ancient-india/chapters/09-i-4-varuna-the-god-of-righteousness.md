<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-55"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="55" data-folio="17" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n54_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 17">[p. 17]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1"><em>VARUNA, THE GOD OF RIGHTEOUSNESS.</em></h2><h3 class="sec-h2">I.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">In</span> boundless radiance is his birth,<br>
He holds the spacious sky and earth,<br>
The heaven’s blue vault he lifts on high,<br>
And spreads the stars across the sky !<br>
And the broad earth, so boundless and so grand,<br>
It is the work, Varuna ! of thy hand !</div></div><h3 class="sec-h2">2.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Shall I with my humble prayer<br>
Before this mighty God appear ?<br>
Will he in his mercy take<br>
The gifts a mortal dares to make ?<br>
With mind from sins and impure passions free,<br>
Oh when shall I his radiant visage see ?</div></div><h3 class="sec-h2">3.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">With anxious thought to thee I turn,<br>
Teach me, Lord ! that I may learn,<br>
What mighty sin pollutes my heart,<br>
And racks me with a cruel smart ?<br>
I’ve questioned men of lore to know the way,<br>
And they have told me, Lord ! to turn to thee !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-56"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="56" data-folio="18" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n55_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 18">[p. 18]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">4.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Teach me, Lord ! what sin unknown<br>
Hath its shadow o’er me thrown ?<br>
Wilt thou in thy anger smite<br>
Thy worshipper, O Lord of light ?<br>
Teach me, Lord ! to expiate my sin,<br>
Teach me, Lord ! to turn to thee again !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">5.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Gracious Monarch ! make us freed<br>
From the sins our fathers did ;<br>
Help to expiate and shun<br>
Unholy acts that we have done !<br>
Like tethered cattle, or like trembling thief,<br>
I tremble, Lord ! But thou canst save my life !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Error often leads to sin,<br>
Wine and anger lead to ruin,<br>
Dice and gambling to distress,<br>
And folly to unrighteousness !<br>
The elder oft the younger will mislead,<br>
And even our dreams unholy thoughts will breed !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">7.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Then save me, Lord ! for thou canst save,<br>
Protect thy worshipper, thy slave.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-57"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="57" data-folio="19" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n56_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 19">[p. 19]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Freed from sin, and strong in mind,<br>
I’ll serve Varuna, ever kind !<br>
Grant us, Aryan <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-09-fn-001" href="#sec-09-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-09-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> God ! light on our path,<br>
And grant us strength, for we are weak in faith !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">8.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Accept, Varuna ! in thy grace,<br>
These humble but these pious lays,<br>
And may it ever pleasing be<br>
The hymn we humbly sing to thee !<br>
And may thy worshippers, with virtue blest,<br>
Find bliss in everything, and peace and rest !</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Rig Veda</em>, VII. 86.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-09-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-09-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body"><em>Devo Aryo</em> in the original text.</span></li></ol></section>
