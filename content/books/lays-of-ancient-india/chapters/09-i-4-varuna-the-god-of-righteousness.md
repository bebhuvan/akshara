<span class="line">Will he in his mercy take</span><br>
<span class="line">The gifts a mortal dares to make?</span><br>
<span class="line">With mind from sins and impure passions free,</span><br>
<span class="line">Oh when shall I his radiant visage see?</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>

<span class="line">What mighty sin pollutes my heart,</span><br>
<span class="line">And racks me with a cruel smart?</span><br>
<span class="line">I’ve questioned men of lore to know the way,</span><br>

<span class="line">Wilt thou in thy anger smite</span><br>
<span class="line">Thy worshipper, O Lord of light?</span><br>
<span class="line">Teach me, Lord! to expiate my sin,</span><br>
