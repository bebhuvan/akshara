<span class="line">How Kuru’s monarch ruled his men,</span><br>
<span class="line">A forester, his task performed,</span><br>
<span class="line">Yudhishthir met in Dvaita’s glen.</span><br>
<span class="line">He bowed and spoke, nor pained his bosom</span><br>
<span class="line">Of the foeman’s rule to tell,</span><br>
<span class="line">Nor seek those men with accents false</span><br>
<span class="line">To please our ear who wish us well.</span><br>
<span class="line">On vengeful thoughts intent, the monarch</span><br>
<span class="line">Bade the spy his tale unfold;</span><br>
<span class="line">And sweet in language, rich in import,</span><br>
<span class="line">Clear in sense, the words he told. (3)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">“Great monarchs see but through their spies,</span><br>
<span class="line">And spies should ne’er deceive their lord;</span><br>
<span class="line">Forgive my speech if plain or pleasing,</span><br>
<span class="line">Seldom true is honied word!</span><br>
<span class="line">False friend! who teaches not his king,</span><br>
<span class="line">False king! who hears not what they tell;</span><br>
<span class="line">Where king and friends in truth agree,</span><br>
<span class="line">Fortune’s favours love to dwell!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-170"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="170" data-folio="132" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n169_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 132">[p. 132]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">We foresters are simple men,</span><br>
<span class="line">And hard a king’s designs to trace;</span><br>
<span class="line">And if thy foeman’s plans I ken,</span><br>
<span class="line">’Tis by thy kindness, by thy grace! (6)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">“Seated on his throne he trembles</span><br>
<span class="line">At thee,—dwelling in the wood!</span><br>
<span class="line">The realm he won by trick of dice</span><br>
<span class="line">He rules by righteous laws and good.</span><br>
<span class="line">Deep in wiles, he would surpass thee</span><br>
<span class="line">In his fame for righteousness;</span><br>
<span class="line">Better far to war with true men</span><br>
<span class="line">Than to consort with the base!</span><br>
<span class="line">His passions veiled, by Manu’s laws</span><br>
<span class="line">He seeks the virtuous path to tread;</span><br>
<span class="line">By night and day his task dividing,</span><br>
<span class="line">Seeks a righteous fame to spread.</span><br>
<span class="line">He treats his menials as his friends,</span><br>
<span class="line">And as his kinsmen friends withal;</span><br>
<span class="line">Veiling his wrath he seeks to prove</span><br>
<span class="line">How conquering love rules over all!</span><br>
<span class="line">With love impartial, equal care,</span><br>
<span class="line">He cultivates all virtues well;</span><br>
<span class="line">And by his worth all varied virtues</span><br>
<span class="line">In harmonious concert dwell!</span><br>
<span class="line">His kindness never lacks in gifts,</span><br>
<span class="line">His gifts with courteous manners flow,</span><br>
<span class="line">And his courtesy and his favours</span><br>
<span class="line">Only men of virtue know.</span><br>
<span class="line">Not for wealth and not in anger,</span><br>
<span class="line">Ever seeking righteous cause,</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-171" data-page="171" data-folio="133" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n170_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 133">[p. 133]</button>On his foe or on his children</span><br>
<span class="line">Visits sins with equal laws.</span><br>
<span class="line">Placing trusty guards around him,</span><br>
<span class="line">Ever wears a fearless mien;</span><br>
<span class="line">And wealth bestowed at sacrifices</span><br>
<span class="line">Speaks his gratitude to men.</span><br>
<span class="line">And by careful thought devised,</span><br>
<span class="line">His plans, with steady toil pursued,</span><br>
<span class="line">Open out a prosperous future,</span><br>
<span class="line">And conjointly lead to good! (15)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">“Cars and horses of great chieftains</span><br>
<span class="line">Throng his palace court around;</span><br>
<span class="line">Tuskers sent by mighty monarchs</span><br>
<span class="line">With their perfume<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-41-fn-001" href="#sec-41-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-41-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> moist the ground.</span><br>
<span class="line">And Kuru’s lands are rich in harvests</span><br>
<span class="line">Ripening without tiller’s toil;</span><br>
<span class="line">Ask no rain, since Kuru’s monarch</span><br>
<span class="line">Showers his blessings on the soil.</span><br>
<span class="line">His bounteous and his peaceful rule</span><br>
<span class="line">To plenty and to wealth give birth;</span><br>
<span class="line">Attracted by his godlike worth</span><br>
<span class="line">Spontaneous yields her wealth the earth.</span><br>
<span class="line">Mighty chiefs, by wealth attracted,</span><br>
<span class="line">Warriors famed in many a strife,</span><br>
<span class="line">Against their monarch never muster,</span><br>
<span class="line">Serve his cause even with their life.</span><br>
<span class="line">His task performed, by trusty spies</span><br>
<span class="line">He learns the plans of other kings;</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-172" data-page="172" data-folio="134" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n171_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 134">[p. 134]</button><em>His</em> secret plan is only known,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like Heaven’s designs, by fruits it brings!</span><br>
<span class="line">His bow is never strung nor raised,</span><br>
<span class="line">Nor doth a frown his face o’erspread;</span><br>
<span class="line">His kindly rule the chieftains cherish</span><br>
<span class="line">Like a garland on their head!</span><br>
<span class="line">Strong in his rule, he now hath placed</span><br>
<span class="line">Duhsasan <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-41-fn-002" href="#sec-41-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-41-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> young in kingly power;</span><br>
<span class="line">With offerings due, in holy rites,</span><br>
<span class="line">By Indra told, he worships Fire!</span><br>
<span class="line">His foes are quelled, his future glorious,</span><br>
<span class="line">And his realm extends to sea;</span><br>
<span class="line">But strife with great men ends in sorrow,—</span><br>
<span class="line">And Duryodhan quakes at thee!</span><br>
<span class="line">If thy name is spoke in converse,</span><br>
<span class="line">Quakes at Arjun’s might of arm,</span><br>
<span class="line">Bends in grief his anguished forehead,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like a snake by <em>Mantra’s</em> charm!</span><br>
<span class="line">Prepared he is to do thee battle;</span><br>
<span class="line">Take then, lord, precautions meet;</span><br>
<span class="line">We gather words by others spoken,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Such, I lay them at thy feet.” (25)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">This said, the woodman went away,</span><br>

<span class="line">And the king in Bhîma’s presence</span><br>
<span class="line">To Draupadî told the tale.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-41-fn-003" href="#sec-41-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-41-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup></span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-173"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="173" data-folio="135" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n172_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 135">[p. 135]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">She heard the tale of foemen’s glory,</span><br>
<span class="line">Thought of wrongs which they had done,</span><br>
<span class="line">And to rouse her husband’s anger</span><br>
<span class="line">Drupad’s daughter thus began. (27)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">“Counsel to a saintly monarch</span><br>

<span class="line">But ignoring woman’s duty,</span><br>
<span class="line">Pardon if my feelings speak!</span><br>
<span class="line">Of thy race the godlike monarchs</span><br>
<span class="line">Held their rule o’er all this land;</span><br>
<span class="line">But as tuskers hurl their garlands,</span><br>
<span class="line">Thou hast hurled it from thy hand!</span><br>
<span class="line">Weak are they who with the wily</span><br>
<span class="line">Deal not with responding wile;</span><br>
<span class="line">For like shafts on mail-less warriors,</span><br>
<span class="line">Artful foemen on them steal!</span><br>
<span class="line">Glory, in thy race begotten,</span><br>

<span class="line">Men spontaneous yield to heroes</span><br>

<span class="line">If my lord compassion feels!</span><br>
<span class="line">Arjun, conquering northern nations,</span><br>
<span class="line">To the poor all treasures gave!</span><br>
<span class="line">God-like Arjun, clad in wild barks,</span><br>
<span class="line">Doth he thy compassion move?</span><br>
<span class="line">Does my lord in patience witness</span><br>
<span class="line">Yonder twins,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-41-fn-004" href="#sec-41-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-41-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> so great, so good?—</span><br>

<span class="line">Scarce I guess thy feelings, monarch,</span><br>
