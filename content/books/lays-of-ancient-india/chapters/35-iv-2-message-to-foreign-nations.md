<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-136"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="136" data-folio="98" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n135_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 98">[p. 98]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1"><em>MESSAGE TO FOREIGN NATIONS.</em></h2><h4 class="sec-h3">1.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">Far</span>, far extend Kalinga’s plains,<br>
And Bengal’s fertile coast ;<br>
Asoka, of the gods beloved,<br>
There sent his conquering host.<br>
They slaved a hundred thousand men,<br>
A hundred thousand died,—<br>
“And is this what a conquest means ?”<br>
The pious monarch cried !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">2.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“Brâhmans pure and Srâmans good,<br>
And men who toil in life ;<br>
Parents to their children dear,<br>
Fond husband, weeping wife,—<br>
These I’ve killed, enslaved and sold,<br>
And bade the helpless die !”<br>
A throb was on the monarch’s brow,<br>
A tear was in his eye !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-137"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="137" data-folio="99" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n136_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 99">[p. 99]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Repentance wrung his royal breast,<br>
And he was meek and lowly ;<br>
And virtuous wishes filled his soul,<br>
And aspirations holy.<br>
He thought of Sâkya, noble Teacher,<br>
Buddha, high of birth,<br>
Who left his throne and toiled for men,<br>
And spread good will on earth !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">4.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“Ho ! other conquests I will spread,<br>
And other trophies win,”—<br>
The pious monarch thus he cried,—<br>
“I’ll conquer crime and sin !<br>
Unto the frontiers of my realm,<br>
And far beyond, proclaim,<br>
Asoka fights in faith alone,<br>
And wins the righteous fame !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">5.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“To Antiochus, Syria’s lord,<br>
To Egypt’s Ptolemy,<br>
To Antigonus, mighty king<br>
Of Macedon the free,</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-138"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="138" data-folio="100" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n137_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 100">[p. 100]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">To Magas of Cyrene fair,<br>
To Alexander, king<br>
Of mountain-covered Epiros,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-35-fn-001" href="#sec-35-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-35-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup>—<br>
The joyful tidings bring !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“To far Kâverî’s fertile banks,<br>
Where mighty Cholas live,<br>
To Kumârikâ’s distant point,<br>
Where Pândya clans still thrive,<br>
To Tâmraparni’s verdant isle,<br>
For pearls well known to fame,<br>
To Henarâja Vismâvasi<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-35-fn-002" href="#sec-35-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-35-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup><br>
The joyful news proclaim !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">7.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“To our friends the Bactrian Greeks,<br>
And to the bold Kambojas,<br>
To Nâbhakas and Nâbhapantis,<br>
And to the sturdy Bhojas,</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-139"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="139" data-folio="101" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n138_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 101">[p. 101]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">To Petenikas in the south,<br>
To Andhras in Deccan,<br>
To far Pulindas tell the news,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-35-fn-003" href="#sec-35-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-35-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup>—<br>
Tell it to every man !”</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">8.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Far and wide the news they told,<br>
And Buddha’s faith proclaimed ;<br>
Strange nations Buddha’s precepts learnt,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-35-fn-004" href="#sec-35-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-35-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup><br>
His holy name they named.<br>
Asoka felt a joy intense,<br>
His life’s great work was done,<br>
For he had won the righteous fame,<br>
