<span class="line"><span class="sc">LIGHTED</span> Agni flames forth high,</span><br>
<span class="line">Flings a radiance on the sky,</span><br>
<span class="line">And his lustre, glorious, bright,</span><br>
<span class="line">Mingles with the morning light.</span><br>
<span class="line">And Visvavârâ chants her holy prayer,</span><br>
<span class="line">Faces the east, and brings her gifts to Fire!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">2.</span><br>
<span class="line">Lord of Eternity! Lord of skies!</span><br>
<span class="line">Presiding Lord of sacrifice!</span><br>
<span class="line">Agni! Whoso worships thee,</span><br>
<span class="line">By him with thy blessings be!</span><br>
<span class="line">Whose blazing altar sacred Agni lights,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Affluence dwells with him and pious rites.</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-59"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="59" data-folio="21" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n58_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 21">[p. 21]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>
<span class="line">Agni! On the altar shine,</span><br>
<span class="line">Flames and radiance bright be thine!</span><br>
<span class="line">Grant us wealth with thy red flame,</span><br>
<span class="line">Quell our foes, increase our fame!</span><br>
<span class="line">Invest our homes with blessings from above,</span><br>
<span class="line">And link our men and wives in bonds of love!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">4.</span><br>
<span class="line">Agni! On the altar shine,</span><br>
<span class="line">Flames and radiance bright be thine!</span><br>
<span class="line">And I lift my pious hymn</span><br>
<span class="line">To thy bright effulgent beam!</span><br>
<span class="line">Bounteous god! Red lustre e’er be thine,</span><br>
<span class="line">Flame on our altar, glorious and divine!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">5.</span><br>
<span class="line">With pious hands we light thy flame,</span><br>
<span class="line">With pious lips we chant thy name!</span><br>
<span class="line">Invoke unto our sacrifice</span><br>
<span class="line">The glorious bright gods of the skies!</span><br>
<span class="line">For thou art Priest<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-10-fn-001" href="#sec-10-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-10-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> in every pious rite,</span><br>
<span class="line">And makest gifts to gods with radiance bright!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-60"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="60" data-folio="22" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n59_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 22">[p. 22]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">6.</span><br>
