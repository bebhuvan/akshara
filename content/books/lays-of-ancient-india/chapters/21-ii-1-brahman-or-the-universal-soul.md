Him, and will mingle with Him.”—<em>Civilisation in Ancient India</em>,<br>
vol. i. p. 191.</p></blockquote></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">1.</span><br>
<span class="line"><span class="sc">This</span> Universe is Brahman’s Self!</span><br>
<span class="line">A part of Him,—these creatures all!</span><br>
<span class="line">In Him their birth, they live in Him,</span><br>
<span class="line">And into Him they end withal!</span><br>
<span class="line">The mortal ever toils and works,</span><br>
<span class="line">And as he sows upon this earth,</span><br>
<span class="line">In virtue’s soil or ways of sin,</span><br>
<span class="line">So reaps he in a future birth!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">2.</span><br>
<span class="line">He is Life,—Intelligence pure!</span><br>
<span class="line">He is Truth and He is Light!</span><br>
<span class="line">His soul pervades the universe,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like ether,—escapes our mortal sight!</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-94" data-page="94" data-folio="56" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n93_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 56">[p. 56]</button>From Him alone all works proceed,</span><br>
<span class="line">All wishes and all feelings spring,</span><br>
<span class="line">Serene and calm, He never speaks,</span><br>
<span class="line">But in Himself holds everything!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>
<span class="line">He is the Self within my heart,</span><br>
<span class="line">The Soul that lives and dwells within,</span><br>
<span class="line">Smaller than the smallest seed,</span><br>
<span class="line">Or kernel of smallest grain!</span><br>
<span class="line">He is the Self within my heart,</span><br>
<span class="line">Greater than the earth and sky,</span><br>
<span class="line">Greater far than all the worlds,</span><br>
<span class="line">Greater than the heaven on high!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">4.</span><br>
<span class="line">From Him alone all works proceed,</span><br>
<span class="line">All wishes and all feelings spring,</span><br>
<span class="line">Serene and calm, He never speaks,</span><br>
<span class="line">But in Himself holds everything!</span><br>
<span class="line">He is the Self within my heart,</span><br>
<span class="line">He is Brahman!—holding all,</span><br>
<span class="line">And when I leave this world,—to Him</span><br>
