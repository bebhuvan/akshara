Commentators draw a distinction between Savitri, the rising or the unrisen sun, and Sûrya, the bright sun of the day.”—<em>Civilisation in Ancient India</em>, vol. i. p. 84.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The following three verses are taken from a somewhat long hymn. The first of these verses is the famous <em>Gayatrî</em>, the morning prayer of the Brâhmans. It has a deep significance, and pious worshippers in invoking the sun invoke the Higher Power whose radiance and glory are outwardly manifested in the sun.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">1.</span><br>
