<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-79"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="79" data-folio="41" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n78_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 41">[p. 41]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1"><em>A BRIDAL HYMN.</em></h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The last six verses of this long hymn have been translated. They are a part of the marriage ritual of the ancient times, and will interest modern readers.</p></div><h4 class="sec-h3">1.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>To the Bride and Bridegroom :</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">Happy</span> man and beauteous bride !<br>
Be this place your home of pride,<br>
Loving man and duteous wife !<br>
In peaceful union pass your life.<br>
May prattling children fill your home with peace,<br>
And lisping babes their grandsire’s bliss increase !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">2.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>The Bride and Bridegroom say :</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">The Lord on us his gifts bestow,<br>
And happy children round us grow !<br>
In peaceful union pass our life,<br>
Loving man and duteous wife !</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>To the Bride :</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">In thy new home auspicious life be thine !<br>
Be good unto our men and to our kine !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-80"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="80" data-folio="42" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n79_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 42">[p. 42]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><h4 class="sec-h3">[<em>To the Bride :</em>]</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Serve thy lord with love and sweetness,<br>
Be thy soft eyes full of brightness,<br>
Be thy beauty ever bright,<br>
Be thy temper ever sweet !<br>
Bear warlike sons ! and worship gods divine !<br>
Be good unto our men and to our kine !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">4.</h4><h4 class="sec-h3">[<em>Prayer to Indra :</em>]</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">In thy mercy, in thy grace,<br>
Grant her wealth and happiness !<br>
A duteous housewife in her love,<br>
May she happy mother prove !<br>
And may ten warlike sons to her be born,<br>
And from her spouse her heart may never turn !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">5.</h4><h4 class="sec-h3">[<em>To the Bride :</em>]</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">A gentle kindness ever bear<br>
To your husband's parents dear ;<br>
Bring within your loving rule<br>
His sisters and his brothers all ;<br>
With queenly grace extend your gentle reign,<br>
Be good unto our cattle and to men !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-81"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="81" data-folio="43" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n80_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 43">[p. 43]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>The Bride and Bridegroom say :</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">All the gods who live above<br>
May they blend our hearts in love,<br>
May each bright celestial power<br>
Bless us in this nuptial hour!<br>
May they link us in a holy bliss,<br>
And fill our peaceful home with happiness!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Rig Veda</em>, X. 85.</p></div>
