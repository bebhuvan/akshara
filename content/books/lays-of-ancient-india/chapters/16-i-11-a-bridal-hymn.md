They are a part of the marriage ritual of the ancient times, and will interest modern readers.</p></div><h2 class="sec-h1">1.</h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>To the Bride and Bridegroom:</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line"><span class="sc">Happy</span> man and beauteous bride!</span><br>
<span class="line">Be this place your home of pride,</span><br>
<span class="line">Loving man and duteous wife!</span><br>
<span class="line">In peaceful union pass your life.</span><br>
<span class="line">May prattling children fill your home with peace,</span><br>
<span class="line">And lisping babes their grandsire’s bliss increase!</span></div></div><h2 class="sec-h1">2.</h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>The Bride and Bridegroom say:</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">The Lord on us his gifts bestow,</span><br>
<span class="line">And happy children round us grow!</span><br>
<span class="line">In peaceful union pass our life,</span><br>
<span class="line">Loving man and duteous wife!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>To the Bride:</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">In thy new home auspicious life be thine!</span><br>
<span class="line">Be good unto our men and to our kine!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-80"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="80" data-folio="42" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n79_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 42">[p. 42]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">3.</h2><h2 class="sec-h1">[<em>To the Bride:</em>]</h2><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">Serve thy lord with love and sweetness,</span><br>
<span class="line">Be thy soft eyes full of brightness,</span><br>
<span class="line">Be thy beauty ever bright,</span><br>
<span class="line">Be thy temper ever sweet!</span><br>
<span class="line">Bear warlike sons! and worship gods divine!</span><br>
<span class="line">Be good unto our men and to our kine!</span></div></div><h2 class="sec-h1">4.</h2><h2 class="sec-h1">[<em>Prayer to Indra:</em>]</h2><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">In thy mercy, in thy grace,</span><br>
<span class="line">Grant her wealth and happiness!</span><br>
<span class="line">A duteous housewife in her love,</span><br>
<span class="line">May she happy mother prove!</span><br>
<span class="line">And may ten warlike sons to her be born,</span><br>
<span class="line">And from her spouse her heart may never turn!</span></div></div><h2 class="sec-h1">5.</h2><h2 class="sec-h1">[<em>To the Bride:</em>]</h2><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">A gentle kindness ever bear</span><br>
<span class="line">To your husband’s parents dear;</span><br>
<span class="line">Bring within your loving rule</span><br>
<span class="line">His sisters and his brothers all;</span><br>
<span class="line">With queenly grace extend your gentle reign,</span><br>
<span class="line">Be good unto our cattle and to men!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-81"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="81" data-folio="43" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n80_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 43">[p. 43]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1">6.</h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">[<em>The Bride and Bridegroom say:</em>]</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">All the gods who live above</span><br>
<span class="line">May they blend our hearts in love,</span><br>
<span class="line">May each bright celestial power</span><br>
<span class="line">Bless us in this nuptial hour!</span><br>
<span class="line">May they link us in a holy bliss,</span><br>
<span class="line">And fill our peaceful home with happiness!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Rig Veda</em>, X. 85.</p></div>
