Ever dear,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-13-fn-001" href="#sec-13-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-13-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
With thee, we to fields repair!<br>
We will till the grateful field,<br>
Copious harvests it will yield!<br>
Well fed and strong our bullocks and our steeds,<br>
And Kshetrapati will supply our needs.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">2.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Copious milk our cows supply,<br>
And Kshetrapati, from the sky,<br>
Sends us copious, grateful rain,<br>
Sweet to tillers of the plain!<br>
Sing to the god of tillage, ever dear,<br>
He grants us crops and listens to our prayer!</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">May our crops in sweetness grow,<br>
In sweetness may the waters flow.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-70"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="70" data-folio="32" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n69_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 32">[p. 32]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Kshetrapati ! power divine !<br>
Grace and sweetness, they are thine !<br>
With grateful songs and hearts we follow thee,<br>
And let our homes and fields from harm be free !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">4.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Gladly works the merry swain,<br>
And glad the mighty bullocks strain ;<br>
Gladly o’er the yielding soil<br>
The shining ploughshare marks their toil !<br>
Merrily fasten all the traces strong,<br>
Merrily ply the goad and drive along !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">5.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Let us raise our grateful song<br>
To Suna, Sîra, bright and strong.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-13-fn-002" href="#sec-13-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-13-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Ye are gods to tillers dear,<br>
Listen to their humble prayer !<br>
And the bright rain you store within the sky<br>
Will feed the grateful earth as crops grow high !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Auspicious Sîtâ ! beauteous born !<br>
Goddess of the shining corn !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-71"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="71" data-folio="33" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n70_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 33">[p. 33]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Listen to our grateful lays,<br>
Grant to us thy helpful grace !<br>
Grant us, Sîtâ ! wealth and happiness,<br>
Grant us, goddess ! plenteous crops and bliss.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-13-fn-003" href="#sec-13-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-13-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">7.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Indra takes her by the hand,<br>
Pûshan leads her o’er the land,<br>
From the sky obedient drops,<br>
At her bidding feed our crops,<br>
And as the harvest cometh year by year,<br>
With plenteous crops, O goddess ! still appear !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">8.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">The merry ploughshare marks the plain,<br>
And merry work the beasts and men !<br>
And Parjanya, the god of rain,<br>
With grateful showers will feed the plain !<br>
