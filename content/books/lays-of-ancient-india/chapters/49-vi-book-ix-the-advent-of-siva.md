<span class="line">Once more began his pious rites,</span><br>
<span class="line">And worship paid to saintly Siva</span><br>
<span class="line">Dwelling in far Kailâsa’s heights.</span><br>
<span class="line">Firm in his purpose, pure in heart,</span><br>
<span class="line">Unwearied in the sun’s hot ray,</span><br>
<span class="line">In rigid fasts, in penance long</span><br>
<span class="line">The pious chief passed many a day.</span><br>
<span class="line">Mortifying flesh and sense,</span><br>
<span class="line">In penance long he passed his hours,</span><br>
<span class="line">In pious rites, unmoved as rock,</span><br>
<span class="line">For high resolve hath wondrous power!</span><br>
<span class="line">Luscious fruits that ripened near him,</span><br>
<span class="line">Crystal rills that rippled by,—</span><br>
<span class="line">For righteous is sweetest nectar,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Drew from him nor wish nor sigh!</span><br>
<span class="line">He boasted not, he ne’er despaired,</span><br>
<span class="line">He never ceased from righteous toil,</span><br>
<span class="line">Nor wrath nor passions in his heart</span><br>
<span class="line">His noble steadfast faith could soil.</span><br>
<span class="line">He wore a world subduing power,</span><br>
<span class="line">Though pale with many a rigid rite,</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-236" data-page="236" data-folio="198" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n235_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 198">[p. 198]</button>And saints beheld him with a fear,—</span><br>
<span class="line">The great in heart are great in might! (6)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">Brighter than the nightly fires</span><br>
<span class="line">His radiance seemed the woods to fill,</span><br>
<span class="line">More mighty than the boundless sea,</span><br>
<span class="line">And loftier than the towering hill!</span><br>
<span class="line">His sacred <em>mantras</em> ever chanting,</span><br>
<span class="line">With a beauteous light he shone;</span><br>
<span class="line">And on his features fell a radiance</span><br>
<span class="line">Like the halo of the sun!</span><br>
<span class="line">Clad in armour dark, he wore</span><br>
<span class="line">His mighty bow across his chest;</span><br>
<span class="line">So wears the lofty wood-clad hill</span><br>
<span class="line">The glorious rainbow on his breast!</span><br>
<span class="line">When for ablutions,—fixed by rules,—</span><br>
<span class="line">He walked betimes, in morning’s hour,</span><br>
<span class="line">The mountain felt his mighty tread,</span><br>
<span class="line">For worth is might and worth is power!</span><br>
<span class="line">A wondrous lustre on him shone</span><br>
<span class="line">When Arjun stood serene and high,—</span><br>
<span class="line">It shone across the firmament,</span><br>
<span class="line">And flashed upon the upper sky!</span><br>
<span class="line">And on moonless nights there fell</span><br>
<span class="line">Upon the prince a wondrous ray,—</span><br>
<span class="line">And like the silver beams of moon</span><br>
<span class="line">It pierced the darkness of the sky!</span><br>
<span class="line">So bright, so clear the light celestial</span><br>
<span class="line">That the paléd orb of sun</span><br>
<span class="line">Marching through the cloudless sky</span><br>
<span class="line">Scarce with wonted radiance shone! (13)</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-237" data-page="237" data-folio="199" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n236_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 199">[p. 199]</button>Holy hermits viewed in awe</span><br>
<span class="line">His crimson locks, his bow unbent!</span><br>
<span class="line">They whispered,—“Is it Siva’s self</span><br>
<span class="line">On mighty Asur’s death intent?</span><br>
<span class="line">Is he great Indra or the Sun?</span><br>
<span class="line">Or Fire, who helps our pious rites?</span><br>
<span class="line">No mortal he of woman born,</span><br>
<span class="line">Such glory decks no anchorites.”</span><br>
<span class="line">But unlike the tongues of Fire</span><br>
<span class="line">Serene was Arjun’s radiant light;</span><br>
<span class="line">Unlike the scorching rays of Sun,</span><br>
<span class="line">’Twas gentle in its wondrous might!</span><br>
<span class="line">As virtues seek true gentleness,</span><br>
<span class="line">As moral rules seek peaceful thought,</span><br>
<span class="line">As righteous laws seek precepts pure,</span><br>
<span class="line">The troubled saints great Siva sought! (17)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">Dazzled, blinded, when they came,</span><br>

<span class="line">To compass Siva’s glorious might!</span><br>
<span class="line">In humble prayer they sought the Lord</span><br>

<span class="line">And by its grace they faintly saw</span><br>
<span class="line">His glorious mien, his triple eye!</span><br>
<span class="line">Resting on his sacred bull,</span><br>
<span class="line">His glorious arm of wondrous might,</span><br>
<span class="line">Sweet Umâ’s lord, to Umâ dear,</span><br>
<span class="line">Stood forth upon the mountain’s height!</span><br>
<span class="line">Far distant from the living world</span><br>
<span class="line">He stood upon a snowy height;</span><br>

<span class="line">Felt his presence and his might!</span><br>

<span class="line">Caught its dark and tremulous glow!</span><br>
<span class="line">By his tresses partly hid,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like Gangâ’s ripples looked the moon,</span><br>
<span class="line">And on his fair and ample forehead</span><br>
