<span class="line">Attended by Gandharvas skilled,</span><br>
<span class="line">And joyous notes of drum and shell</span><br>
<span class="line">And sound of cars the blue vault filled.</span><br>
<span class="line">And eager denizens of the sky</span><br>
<span class="line">Poured forth to greet them as they went;</span><br>
<span class="line">The orb of day with upward beams</span><br>
<span class="line">Upon the nymphs his radiance lent.</span><br>
<span class="line">The toil of the unwonted march,</span><br>
<span class="line">The blowing breeze, the sun’s bright shine,</span><br>
<span class="line">Crimsoned their blushing cheeks and brow</span><br>
<span class="line">As with the gentle flush of wine!</span><br>
<span class="line">Celestial coursers fresh and powerful</span><br>
<span class="line">Drew the cars with speed of wind;</span><br>
<span class="line">Scarce the wheels did seem to roll,</span><br>
<span class="line">But sky and space flew quick behind!</span><br>
<span class="line">From regions far, close to the sun,</span><br>
<span class="line">Where his red rays are bright and strong,</span><br>
<span class="line">Far far they went, where like a braid</span><br>
<span class="line">Celestial Gangâ rolls along.</span><br>
<span class="line">Cooling zephyrs, gently blowing,</span><br>
<span class="line">Saturate with Gangâ’s spray,</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-216" data-page="216" data-folio="178" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n215_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 178">[p. 178]</button>Scented by the heavenly lotus,</span><br>
<span class="line">Cheered the damsels on their way.</span><br>
<span class="line">And in many a fold the clouds</span><br>
<span class="line">Spanned the space ’twixt earth and sky,</span><br>
<span class="line">And the steeds by drivers checked</span><br>
<span class="line">Lighted on the earth from high.</span><br>
<span class="line">The scented woods of Himalay,</span><br>
<span class="line">Blooming creepers, beauteous trees,</span><br>
<span class="line">Hailed the heavenly nymphs with joy,</span><br>
<span class="line">Honoured by their matchless grace! (8)</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="line">And now the nymphs in quest of flowers</span><br>
<span class="line">Wandered through the woodland way,</span><br>
<span class="line">Unheeding many a shrub and blossom,</span><br>
<span class="line">So thick the flowers, so merry they!</span><br>
<span class="line">The maddened bee forsook the bush</span><br>
<span class="line">To cull the nectar from their arms;</span><br>
<span class="line">Their pink white hands were like young shoots,</span><br>
<span class="line">Their fingers wore the blossom’s charms!</span><br>
<span class="line">Sweetly flowed the mountain rill,</span><br>
<span class="line">Her sands were robed with Kusa chaste,</span><br>
<span class="line">And graceful strings of tuneful ducks</span><br>
<span class="line">Like chain of gold bedecked her waist!</span><br>
<span class="line">Cascades in silver masses fell,</span><br>
<span class="line">And spread afar in ripples bright;</span><br>
<span class="line">And all the wild woods seemed to smile</span><br>
<span class="line">With the spray so pearly white.</span><br>
<span class="line">And creepers oped their gentle blossoms</span><br>
<span class="line">Decked with strings of dark blue bees,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like tender-hearted gentle maidens</span><br>
<span class="line">With their soft and trustful eyes! (13)</span><br>

<span class="line">As through the woods the damsels moved,</span><br>
<span class="line">With female art, for needless help,</span><br>
<span class="line">They asked the fond Gandharvas<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-47-fn-001" href="#sec-47-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-47-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> loved.</span><br>
<span class="line">Some nymph with lifted, blushing face,</span><br>
<span class="line">Still hung on soft tales whispered nigh;</span><br>
<span class="line">Her idle hand no blossom culled,</span><br>
<span class="line">The loosened robe forgot to tie!</span><br>
<span class="line">Some with flower-buds decked her ringlets,</span><br>
<span class="line">With her beauteous tapering arms,</span><br>
<span class="line">And scarce rebuked the loving swain</span><br>
<span class="line">Who gently clasped her swelling charms!</span><br>
<span class="line">Some sought her lover’s gentle aid</span><br>
<span class="line">To blow the pollen from her eye,</span><br>
<span class="line">And scarce the lover could regret</span><br>
<span class="line">The sweet mishap that drew him nigh!</span><br>
<span class="line">Laden with flowers the nymphs returned</span><br>

<span class="line">Their velvet cheeks were dewed with toil,</span><br>
<span class="line">Like lotus dimmed with streaks of snow!</span><br>
<span class="line">The gods in rapture viewed the nymphs,</span><br>
<span class="line">Their languid charms, their movements</span><br>
<span class="line">slow! (20)</span><br>

<span class="line">The eager waters clasped the nymphs,</span><br>
