Attended by Gandharvas skilled,<br>
And joyous notes of drum and shell<br>
And sound of cars the blue vault filled.<br>
And eager denizens of the sky<br>
Poured forth to greet them as they went;<br>
The orb of day with upward beams<br>
Upon the nymphs his radiance lent.<br>
The toil of the unwonted march,<br>
The blowing breeze, the sun’s bright shine,<br>
Crimsoned their blushing cheeks and brow<br>
As with the gentle flush of wine!<br>
Celestial coursers fresh and powerful<br>
Drew the cars with speed of wind;<br>
Scarce the wheels did seem to roll,<br>
But sky and space flew quick behind!<br>
From regions far, close to the sun,<br>
Where his red rays are bright and strong,<br>
Far far they went, where like a braid<br>
Celestial Gangâ rolls along.<br>
Cooling zephyrs, gently blowing,<br>
Saturate with Gangâ’s spray,</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-216"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="216" data-folio="178" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n215_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 178">[p. 178]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Scented by the heavenly lotus,<br>
Cheered the damsels on their way.<br>
And in many a fold the clouds<br>
Spanned the space ’twixt earth and sky,<br>
And the steeds by drivers checked<br>
Lighted on the earth from high.<br>
The scented woods of Himalay,<br>
Blooming creepers, beauteous trees,<br>
Hailed the heavenly nymphs with joy,<br>
Honoured by their matchless grace ! (8)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">And now the nymphs in quest of flowers<br>
Wandered through the woodland way,<br>
Unheeding many a shrub and blossom,<br>
So thick the flowers, so merry they !<br>
The maddened bee forsook the bush<br>
To cull the nectar from their arms ;<br>
Their pink white hands were like young shoots,<br>
Their fingers wore the blossom’s charms !<br>
Sweetly flowed the mountain rill,<br>
Her sands were robed with Kusa chaste,<br>
And graceful strings of tuneful ducks<br>
Like chain of gold bedecked her waist !<br>
Cascades in silver masses fell,<br>
And spread afar in ripples bright ;<br>
And all the wild woods seemed to smile<br>
With the spray so pearly white.<br>
And creepers oped their gentle blossoms<br>
Decked with strings of dark blue bees,<br>
Like tender-hearted gentle maidens<br>
With their soft and trustful eyes ! (13)</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-217"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="217" data-folio="179" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n216_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 179">[p. 179]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">All around the blossoms hung<br>
As through the woods the damsels moved,<br>
With female art, for needless help,<br>
They asked the fond Gandharvas<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-47-fn-001" href="#sec-47-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-47-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> loved.<br>
Some nymph with lifted, blushing face,<br>
Still hung on soft tales whispered nigh ;<br>
Her idle hand no blossom culled,<br>
The loosened robe forgot to tie !<br>
Some with flower-buds decked her ringlets,<br>
With her beauteous tapering arms,<br>
And scarce rebuked the loving swain<br>
Who gently clasped her swelling charms !<br>
Some sought her lover’s gentle aid<br>
To blow the pollen from her eye,<br>
And scarce the lover could regret<br>
The sweet mishap that drew him nigh !<br>
Laden with flowers the nymphs returned<br>
From flowering woods,—a merry band,<br>
Their weary feet scarce knew the way,<br>
And often slipped on even land !<br>
Their girdles rich in sparkling gems,<br>
Hung loose below their slender waist ;<br>
The loosened zone proclaimed their languor,<br>
And their panting heaving breast !<br>
Their velvet cheeks were dewed with toil,<br>
Like lotus dimmed with streaks of snow !<br>
The gods in rapture viewed the nymphs,<br>
Their languid charms, their movements<br>
slow ! The languid damsels slow repair;<br>
Where fishes glance through lilies blown,<br>
And notes of wild fowl fill the air.<br>
The eager waters clasped the nymphs,<br>
And played with garlands on their hair,<br>
And rashly washed their Chandain paints,<br>
In little wavelets fled afar!<br>
Some damsel hid, and others searched,<br>
Are those her eyes or lotus bright?<br>
Is that her hair or string of bees?<br>
Are those her teeth or lilies sweet?<br>
Some nymph on others water threw,<br>
Or washed and wet, yet lovelier shone!<br>
Her face was flushed, her bosom heaved,<br>
Scarce held her robe the loosened zone!<br>
The sportive nymphs arose at last,<br>
Like rain-washed lilies doubly bright,<br>
Still sparkling with the glistening drops,<br>
And radiant as the starry night! (25)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">So closed the day, and darkness fell<br>
O’er stream and forest, far and nigh,<br>
And beauty left the things of earth<br>
And shone upon the starry sky.<br>
Until the brightening eastern heaven,<br>
Streaked with silver, faintly shone,<br>
And pure and white as Ketak flower<br>
Spread the light of rising moon!<br>
Slowly rolled the flood of light<br>
O’er the dark and nightly sky,</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-219"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="219" data-folio="181" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n218_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 181">[p. 181]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Like the Gangâ’s ruddy wave<br>
Mingling with the azure sea!<br>
Like a disk of burnished gold,<br>
Flooding earth and sky with light,<br>
From the distant eastern main<br>
Rose the glorious Lord of night!<br>
And he drew the veil of gloom<br>
From the face of earth so wide,<br>
As a kinsman draws the veil<br>
For to bless a bashful bride! (30)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">At dawn the heavenly nymphs arose,<br>
Decked themselves with jewels rare;<br>
Went to Arjun’s hermitage<br>
With sweet glances, graces rare!<br>
On their feet the tinkling bells,<br>
On their waists the sounding chain;<br>
With soft music filled the woods<br>
Till the birds sang back the strain!<br>
Pale with penances and rites,<br>
Clad in arms, but calm and great,<br>
Peaceful as the mighty Vedas,<br>
Arjun great at last they met!<br>
Radiant in a robe of light<br>
On the lofty hill he stood,<br>
Like the beauteous lord of night<br>
Seemed the lord of all the wood!<br>
Pale with penances,—but great,<br>
Warlike,—in his peaceful bower,<br>
Alone,—but strong as hosts in war,<br>
A saint,—but wielding Indra’s power! Slow the artful damsels come,<br>
Conscious of their matchless beauty,<br>
Vain they know his rites and aim !<br>
Slow and soft a music soundeth<br>
Through the glades and o'er the<br>
hills ;<br>
Every season brings its blessings,<br>
And the wood with rapture fills !<br>
Round the hermitage of Arjun<br>
Mâlatîs in blossoms grow ;<br>
Washed by rains the lotus blossoms<br>
With a lovelier freshness blow !<br>
On the luscious berries feasting<br>
Kokils sing their joyous lay,<br>
Gladdening e'en the mourner's bosom<br>
With their wondrous melody !<br>
Breezes blow through sweet Kadambas,<br>
Wafting peacocks' notes of love,<br>
Gently fanning pious Arjun<br>
From his rites his heart to move !<br>
Mango blossoms lend their fragrance,<br>
Cooling zephyrs gently move,<br>
Spring and winter move conjointly<br>
Wakening gentle thoughts of love !<br>
On the jasmine's opening blossoms,<br>
Shaken by the gentle breeze,<br>
Sweet as quivering lips of maidens,<br>
Love's sweet kisses press the bees !<br>
Jasmine sweet nor lilies scented<br>
Ope so sweet, when darkness flies,</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-221"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="221" data-folio="183" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n220_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 183">[p. 183]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">As upon the saintly Arjun<br>
Oped the nymphs their merry eyes ! (43)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Before the saints the nymphs appear,<br>
And lightly step upon the grass ;<br>
Their tender feet vermilion painted<br>
On the heather gently press !<br>
Some nymph behind her maid conceals—<br>
From coyness, or from female art,—<br>
Her sidelong glance on Arjun sends,<br>
Betraying feelings of her heart !<br>
Another frolics on the meadow<br>
In her blooming loveliness ;<br>
The wanton zephyrs oft disclose<br>
Her budding charms, bewitching grace !<br>
Some damsel resteth on the green,<br>
On her fair hand her pale face raising,<br>
And with her soft and languid eyes<br>
Upon the beauteous hero gazing.<br>
Another sings with thirsty lips<br>
And frequent sighs some ancient lay,<br>
And her soft eyes and heaving bosom<br>
Speak the words she cannot say !<br>
Some sprightly nymph beside the tree<br>
From her sweet and laughing eyes<br>
Sends on Arjun tender darts,<br>
Rapid as the arrow flies !<br>
Another damsel scours the plain,<br>
Her loosened robes held on her breast<br>
But scarce her nimble feet can move,<br>
The slipping zone restrains her haste !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-222"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="222" data-folio="184" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n221_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 184">[p. 184]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">But vain these blandishments and charms,<br>
And vain each winning female art !<br>
Still steadfast in its lofty faith,<br>
Unmoved like rock is Arjun’s heart ! (51)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Attended by their swains the nymphs withdrew,<br>
And Arjun, to his purpose ever true,<br>
With pious rites, and faith, ennobling, high,<br>
In prayers sought the Lord of earth and sky.
