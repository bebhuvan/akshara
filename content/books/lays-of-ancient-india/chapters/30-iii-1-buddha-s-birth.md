<span class="line">Asita the Rishi found</span><br>
<span class="line">Gods in all the sky assembled,</span><br>
<span class="line">Wafting blessings, pleased in mind!</span><br>
<span class="line">“Wherefore are they all assembled,</span><br>
<span class="line">Wave their garments in the sky?</span><br>
<span class="line">Wherefore,” asked the mighty Rishi,</span><br>
<span class="line">“Pleased in mind the gods on high?”</span><br>
<span class="line">“’Tis because the saintly Master</span><br>
<span class="line">For the good of all is born,</span><br>
<span class="line">He is born among the Sâkyas,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-30-fn-001" href="#sec-30-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-30-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup></span><br>
<span class="line">Fair Lumbini doth adorn!”</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">2.</span><br>
<span class="line">Asita, the mighty Rishi,</span><br>
<span class="line">Went unto the Sâkya king,</span><br>
<span class="line">“Unto thee a babe is given,—</span><br>
<span class="line">I to him my homage bring!”</span><br>
<span class="line"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger folio-turn" id="page-124" data-page="124" data-folio="86" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n123_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect original scan for page 86">[p. 86]</button>They brought the babe, like gold resplendent,</span><br>
<span class="line">Wrought and shaped by skilful hand,</span><br>
<span class="line">Beaming with an inward glory,</span><br>
<span class="line">Jewel of the blessed land!</span><br>
<span class="line">“He is the auspicious Master,”</span><br>
<span class="line">So the sage Asita said,</span><br>
<span class="line">“He will be the great <em>awakened</em>,</span><br>
<span class="line">Rule of righteousness will spread.”</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>
<span class="line">And the blind received their sight,</span><br>
<span class="line">Longing for the babe to see!</span><br>
<span class="line">And the deaf they heard again,</span><br>
<span class="line">Heard his words of piety!</span><br>
<span class="line">And the dumb they spoke again</span><br>
