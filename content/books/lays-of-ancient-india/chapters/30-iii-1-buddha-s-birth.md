Asita the Rishi found<br>
Gods in all the sky assembled,<br>
Wafting blessings, pleased in mind!<br>
“Wherefore are they all assembled,<br>
Wave their garments in the sky?<br>
Wherefore,” asked the mighty Rishi,<br>
“Pleased in mind the gods on high?”<br>
“’Tis because the saintly Master<br>
For the good of all is born,<br>
He is born among the Sâkyas,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-30-fn-001" href="#sec-30-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-30-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Fair Lumbini doth adorn!”</div></div><h3 class="sec-h2">2.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Asita, the mighty Rishi,<br>
Went unto the Sâkya king,<br>
“Unto thee a babe is given,—<br>
I to him my homage bring!”</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-124"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="124" data-folio="86" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n123_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 86">[p. 86]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">They brought the babe, like gold resplendent,<br>
Wrought and shaped by skilful hand,<br>
Beaming with an inward glory,<br>
Jewel of the blessed land !<br>
“ He is the auspicious Master,”<br>
So the sage Asita said,<br>
“ He will be the great <em>awakened</em>,<br>
Rule of righteousness will spread.”</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">And the blind received their sight,<br>
Longing for the babe to see !<br>
And the deaf they heard again,<br>
Heard his words of piety !<br>
And the dumb they spoke again<br>
Of the saintly Master come !<br>
And the crooked men were straight,<br>
Walked forth free the halt and lame !<br>
Chains and shackles broke and fell<br>
In the prisons of the earth !<br>
And the blue and joyous sky<br>
Hailed the saintly Master’s birth !</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>From the Nâlaka Sutta and Birth Stories.</em></p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-30-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-30-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">Kapilavastu, the capital town of the Sâkya clan, was situated on the banks of the Rohini river, to the north-east of modern Behar.
