<span class="line"><span class="sc">Gods</span> and men and lower beings,—</span><br>
<span class="line">All from mighty Brahman flow!</span><br>
<span class="line">But in vanity and blindness</span><br>
<span class="line">Brahman great, we do not know!</span><br>
<span class="line">Once in past and ancient ages</span><br>
<span class="line">Vanity the gods o’ercame,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Boastful of their power and glory,</span><br>
<span class="line">Knew not whence that prowess came!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">2.</span><br>
<span class="line">Brahman to the gods appeared,</span><br>
<span class="line">And they knew not who was He,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Unto Agni thus they spake,—</span><br>
<span class="line">“Know thou who this stranger be.”</span><br>
<span class="line">Brahman placed a blade of straw,</span><br>
<span class="line">And the mighty god of Fire</span><br>
<span class="line">Vainly strove to burn the blade,</span><br>
<span class="line">Went back in his shame and ire!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-109"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="109" data-folio="71" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n108_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 71">[p. 71]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>
<span class="line">Unto Vâyu, god of tempests,</span><br>
<span class="line">Strong in might, resistless, free,</span><br>
<span class="line">Spake the gods in secret terror,</span><br>
<span class="line">“Know thou who this stranger be.”</span><br>
<span class="line">Brahman placed a blade of light straw,</span><br>
<span class="line">On it mighty Vâyu came,</span><br>
<span class="line">Vain he strove the blade to carry,</span><br>
<span class="line">Went back in his ire and shame!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">4.</span><br>
<span class="line">Unto Indra, king of bright gods,</span><br>
<span class="line">Highest in the realms or sky,</span><br>
<span class="line">Spake the gods with soft entreaty,</span><br>
<span class="line">“Know thou who this stranger be.”</span><br>
<span class="line">Indra unto Brahman came,</span><br>
<span class="line">Knew not Brahman in his might,</span><br>
<span class="line">Saw no stranger god before him,—</span><br>
<span class="line">He had vanished from his sight!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">5.</span><br>
<span class="line">Radiant in her god-like splendour,</span><br>
<span class="line">Umâ Haimavatî came!</span><br>
<span class="line">Umâ of the northern mountains,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Heavenly lore in mortal frame!</span><br>
<span class="line">Of her Indra humbly questioned,</span><br>
<span class="line">Who the stranger god might be,—</span><br>
<span class="line">To him Umâ gently answered,</span><br>
<span class="line">God of all the gods was He!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-110"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="110" data-folio="72" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n109_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 72">[p. 72]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">6.</span><br>
<span class="line">“As the lightning’s mighty splendour</span><br>
<span class="line">Fills the eye with dazzling light,</span><br>
<span class="line">Knowledge of the mighty Brahman</span><br>
<span class="line">Fills the heart with radiance bright!</span><br>
<span class="line">From Him flows, ye gods! your prowess,</span><br>
<span class="line">From Him worlds and oceans broad,</span><br>
<span class="line">To Him lift your humble prayers,</span><br>
<span class="line">He is Brahman, He is God.”</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Abridged from Kena Upanishad</em>, III., V.</p></div>
