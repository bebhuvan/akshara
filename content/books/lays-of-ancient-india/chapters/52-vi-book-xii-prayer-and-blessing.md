The Lord of hosts in ashes smeared ;<br>
He views the crescent in his locks,<br>
And bows before the God revered !<br>
His arrows and his mighty bow<br>
Appear once more before his sight !<br>
Himself he finds in armour clad,<br>
And in its case the falchion bright !<br>
Clouds pour forth a gentle rain,<br>
Skies send heavenly blossoms fair,<br>
And a gentle heavenly music<br>
Floats upon the fragrant air !<br>
And the gods by Indra bidden<br>
Gather in their realm on high,<br>
And their chariots gem-bespangled<br>
Are like bright stars in the sky !<br>
Heavenly swans with tinkling bells,<br>
Attendants on the gods on high,<br>
With their softly waving plumage<br>
Gently sail across the sky !<br>
His purpose done, his wish obtained,<br>
Low, low, his forehead Arjun laid,<br>
And unto the God of gods<br>
Thus in humble accents prayed. (6)</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-257"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="257" data-folio="219" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n256_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 219">[p. 219]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">1.<br>
<em>“The world’s Asylum ! Lord of grace !</em><br>
<em>Whom men by worship may attain,</em><br>
<em>Saints by Thy grace have conquered death,</em><br>
<em>And showed the path to gods and men !</em></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">2.<br>
<em>“Who seeks Thee not in loving worship</em><br>
<em>Is a prey to sin and death !</em><br>
<em>A prey to fleeting world’s illusions,</em><br>
<em>Mortals shun his hated breath !</em></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">3.<br>
<em>“Who seeks Thee with a loving worship,</em><br>
<em>Shunning world’s deceitful ways,</em><br>
<em>He finds in Thee his true salvation,</em><br>
<em>Lord of Mercy ! ’Tis Thy grace !</em></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">4.<br>
<em>“Some seek for pleasures on this earth,</em><br>
<em>Some strive for heavenly joys and bliss,</em><br>
<em>But holy refuge there is none</em><br>
<em>Save Thee ! O Lord of holiness !</em></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">5.<br>
<em>“Who seeks Thee, finds his haven shore,</em><br>
<em>Who shuns Thee, walks the downward path,</em><br>
<em>For this is Law ! ’Tis not Thy anger,</em><br>
<em>Thou art Mercy, void of wrath !</em></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-258"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="258" data-folio="220" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n257_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 220">[p. 220]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ <em>We love Thee with a tainted love,</em><br>
<em>Thy form of grace we may not know,</em><br>
<em>But even thus Thy worship, Lord !</em><br>
<em>Salvation brings to us below.</em></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">7.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ <em>Illusions mock our mortal eyes,</em><br>
<em>Our actions like a shadow flee !</em><br>
<em>Who views but Thee beholds the truth,</em><br>
<em>His acts are true who trusts in Thee !</em></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">8.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ <em>Saintly teachers on this earth</em><br>
<em>They teach us precepts good and great ;</em><br>
<em>Thou alone, with mighty grace,</em><br>
<em>From earthly bonds can liberate !</em></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">9.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ <em>To liberate this troubled earth</em><br>
<em>From sin’s tornado, passion’s storm,</em><br>
<em>Thou wearest forms unto our eyes,—</em><br>
<em>For Thou art Mercy,—void of form !</em></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">10.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ <em>Thou art death and Thou art life,</em><br>
<em>The universe lives in Thy laws ;</em><br>
<em>Thou salvation of the pure,</em><br>
<em>Of mighty causes Thou</em> <span class="sc">First Cause</span> ! ”</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-259"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="259" data-folio="221" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n258_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 221">[p. 221]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Thus the good and pious Arjun<br>
Did great Sambhu’s blessings crave ;<br>
Sambhu to the pious hero<br>
Heavenly arms and blessings gave.<br>
And the lore by Siva given,<br>
Radiant with a crimson flame,<br>
As the sun unto a rain-cloud,<br>
So unto great Arjun came !<br>
Indra and the gods assembled,<br>
Blessed the man of steadfast faith ;<br>
Gave unto him arms celestial,<br>
Showed him glory’s lofty path ! (19)</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">“ Faithful mortal ! be thy wish fulfilled ! ”<br>
Thus Siva spoke, and strength and faith instilled ;<br>
And Arjun came once more to Dvaita’s wood,<br>
And reverent bowed to Yudhishthir the good. (20)</div></div>
