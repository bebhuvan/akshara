<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-133"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="133" data-folio="95" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n132_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 95">[p. 95]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1"><em>MESSAGE TO HIS PEOPLE.</em></h2><h3 class="sec-h2">I.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">Thus</span> speaks royal Piyadasi,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-34-fn-001" href="#sec-34-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-34-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Of the gods beloved,<br>
To his many subject nations,<br>
Peoples he has loved :—<br>
I have carved on rocks and pillars<br>
Rules my men obey,<br>
And my Dharma-mahâmâtras<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-34-fn-002" href="#sec-34-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-34-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup><br>
Point to them the way.</div></div><h3 class="sec-h2">2.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Royal highways in my empire<br>
Are by mangoes shaded,<br>
Wells and inns refresh and cheer<br>
The thirsty and the jaded.<br>
But a mission greater, holier,—<br>
To refresh the soul !<br>
To relieve the thirsty heart,<br>
And comfort bring to all !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-134"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="134" data-folio="96" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n133_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 96">[p. 96]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Amongst my men of every sect,<br>
Amongst the Brâhmans holy,<br>
Amongst all sects of mendicants,<br>
Amongst Nirgranthas <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-34-fn-003" href="#sec-34-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-34-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> lowly,<br>
My Mahâmâtras ever work<br>
For holy righteousness,<br>
They work to show the pious way,<br>
To show the path of peace !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">4.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Would’st thou know that pious way,<br>
That righteous path of peace,—<br>
It is the way that leads to joy,<br>
To joy and holy bliss !<br>
<em>Religion !—’tis another name</em><br>
<em>For Truth and Charity,</em><br>
<em>Religious rite and sacrifice</em><br>
<em>Is Kindness, Purity !</em></div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">5.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Some would spread Religious faith<br>
By strong compulsive laws,—<br>
Gentle lessons and persuasion,<br>
Be still Asoka’s ways !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-135"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="135" data-folio="97" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n134_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 97">[p. 97]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">I value inward inspiration,—<br>
Awakening of the heart,—<br>
Not a formal blind compliance<br>
With rules that we impart.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">6.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">With this view this righteous edict<br>
This year <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-34-fn-004" href="#sec-34-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-34-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> I promulgate,<br>
May it teach my sons and grandsons<br>
And my people great!<br>
May it unto after ages<br>
Like sun and moon endure,<br>
May it in this life, and after,<br>
Righteous bliss secure!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>From Pillar Edict</em>, VIII</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-34-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-34-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">The name assumed by Asoka in all his inscriptions.</span></li><li id="sec-34-fn-002" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-34-fn-002" aria-label="Return to text">†.</a> <span class="fn-body">Officers employed to teach religion and moral precepts to the people.</span></li><li id="sec-34-fn-003" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-34-fn-003" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">The learned Brâhmans were generally orthodox Hindus who kept aloof from Buddhism, the religion of the people. The Nirgranthas were a sect of Jainas who went about unclad, and renounced all worldly things.</span></li><li id="sec-34-fn-004" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-34-fn-004" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">The year is mentioned in the text, being twenty-seven years after Asoka's coronation.
