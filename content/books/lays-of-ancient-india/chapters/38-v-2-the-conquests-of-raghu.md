<div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-150"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="150" data-folio="112" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n149_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 112">[p. 112]</button></div><h2 class="sec-h1"><em>THE CONQUESTS OF RAGHU.</em></h2><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p">The piece translated below is important, as it throws light on the geography of India at the time of the poet, <em>i.e.</em>, in the sixth century after Christ.</p></div><h4 class="sec-h3">1.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">Eastward</span> marched Ayodhyâ’s monarch,<br>
Conquering many a sturdy foe,<br>
Till he reached the mighty ocean,<br>
On whose shores the palm trees grow.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">2.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Suhma’s<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-001" href="#sec-38-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> monarch saved his kingdom,<br>
Bowing to the conqueror brave,—<br>
Supple cane-twigs bend as meekly<br>
’Neath the strong resistless wave.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Bengal’s kings came with their war-boats,<br>
Bravely fought, but fought in vain ;<br>
And the victor built a pillar<br>
Where great Gangâ meets the main.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-151"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="151" data-folio="113" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n150_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 113">[p. 113]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">4.<br>
Rice transplanted yields a harvest!<br>
So unto the victor brave,<br>
Conquered kings to thrones restoréd<br>
Presents and rich offerings gave.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">5.<br>
Tuskers formed a bridge most wondrous<br>
O’er the broad Kapisâ’s stream ;<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-002" href="#sec-38-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Utkal’s monarch<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-003" href="#sec-38-fn-003" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-003" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup> led the victor<br>
To Kalinga’s mighty realm.<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-004" href="#sec-38-fn-004" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-004" role="doc-noteref">‡</a></sup></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">6.<br>
Fiercely fought Kalinga’s monarch<br>
On his tusker great in height ;<br>
But in vain his arrows showered<br>
On the victor, proud in might!</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">7.<br>
And on beds of spreading betel<br>
Soldiers sat and drank the wine ;<br>
And Kalinga’s fame and glory<br>
With the wine they seemed to drain!</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-152"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="152" data-folio="114" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n151_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 114">[p. 114]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">8.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">But the great and noble victor<br>
Placed the king again on throne,<br>
Gave him back his wealth and empire,<br>
Robbed him of his fame alone !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">9.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Southward marched Ayodhyâ’s monarch<br>
By the azure sparkling sea,<br>
And through forests of the nut-palm<br>
Marched his soldiers bold and free !</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">10.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Crossed Kâverî, mighty river,<br>
Stopped where steep Malayas rise ;<br>
Steeds they tied in spicy forests,<br>
Tuskers to the sandal trees,</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">11.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Mighty Pândyas,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-005" href="#sec-38-fn-005" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-005" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> brave in combat,<br>
Vainly with great Raghu fought ;<br>
And with pearls from Ceylon’s island<br>
Mighty Raghu’s grace they sought.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-153"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="153" data-folio="115" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n152_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 115">[p. 115]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">12.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Across the lofty Sahya mountains <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-006" href="#sec-38-fn-006" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-006" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Mighty Raghu forced his way,<br>
Far Kerala’s<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-007" href="#sec-38-fn-007" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-007" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup> soft-eyed maidens<br>
Saw his troops and fled away.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">13.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Northwards now Ayodhyâ’s monarch<br>
Went the Persian chiefs <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-008" href="#sec-38-fn-008" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-008" role="doc-noteref">‡</a></sup> to quell ;<br>
Pale-faced Yavan<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-009" href="#sec-38-fn-009" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-009" role="doc-noteref">§</a></sup> women wept<br>
For the chiefs who fought and fell.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">14.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Fiercely fought each western warrior<br>
On his fiery warlike horse ;<br>
But the victor’s swords and arrows<br>
Swept and thinned the hostile force.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-154"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="154" data-folio="116" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n153_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 116">[p. 116]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">15.<br>
Now the victor’s warlike soldiers<br>
Rested in the fields of vine,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-010" href="#sec-38-fn-010" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-010" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Spread the deer-skin on the heather,<br>
Gaily quaffed the fresh grape-wine.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">16.<br>
Northwards then great Raghu travelled,<br>
And his tuskers crossed the sands ;<br>
Scented pollen on them showered,<br>
Wafted from the northern lands.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">17.<br>
Fiery Huns are fierce and warlike,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-011" href="#sec-38-fn-011" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-011" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup>—<br>
Vainly fought with Kosal’s chief !<br>
Women with their ruddy faces<br>
Wept for warriors in their grief.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">18.<br>
Kamboja’s great and sturdy warriors <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-012" href="#sec-38-fn-012" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-012" role="doc-noteref">‡</a></sup><br>
Quailed before Ayodhyâ’s king ;<br>
Gold and fiery steeds of battle<br>
They unto the victor bring.</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-155"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="155" data-folio="117" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n154_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 117">[p. 117]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">19.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Then the hosts of mighty Raghu<br>
Climbed the heights of Himalay,<br>
Beating back the stubborn hill-tribes,<br>
Eastwards now they forced their way.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">20.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">And they crossed great Ganga’s waters,<br>
Crossed Lohityâ’s <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-013" href="#sec-38-fn-013" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-013" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup> sea-like wave;<br>
And the lord of Pragjyotisha <sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-38-fn-014" href="#sec-38-fn-014" data-fn-target="sec-38-fn-014" role="doc-noteref">†</a></sup><br>
Quaked before the victor brave.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">21.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">With the tuskers of his country<br>
Kamrup’s lord had met his foes,—<br>
Now those tuskers came as presents<br>
To where Raghu’s tents arose.</div></div><h4 class="sec-h3">22.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Thus great Raghu, king of monarchs,<br>
Conquering nations far and wide,<br>
Back returned to fair Ayodhyâ,<br>
Ruled with glory and with pride.</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>Abridged from Kâlidâsa’s Raghu-vansa,</em> IV.</p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-38-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-38-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">Supposed to be an ancient kingdom in East Bengal.</span></li><li id="sec-38-fn-002" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-38-fn-002" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">The Suvarnarekhâ river.</span></li><li id="sec-38-fn-003" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-38-fn-003" aria-label="Return to text">†.</a> <span class="fn-body">Northern Orissa.</span></li><li id="sec-38-fn-004" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-38-fn-004" aria-label="Return to text">‡.</a> <span class="fn-body">The great Kalinga kingdom stretched from Orissa to the Krishnâ river in the south.</span></li><li id="sec-38-fn-005" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-38-fn-005" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">One of the three great nations who lived in the extreme south of India.
