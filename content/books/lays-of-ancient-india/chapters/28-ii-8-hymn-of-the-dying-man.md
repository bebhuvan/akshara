They are the last three verses of the Upanishad from which they are translated.</p></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">1.</span><br>
<span class="line"><span class="sc">Sole</span> Rishi! Pushan! glorious Yama!<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-001" href="#sec-28-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup></span><br>
<span class="line">God of day! withdraw thy rays,</span><br>
<span class="line">And let me once more view thy splendour,—</span><br>
<span class="line">View it with my closing eyes!</span><br>
<span class="line">Let me once more view the light,</span><br>
<span class="line">The gladsome light that dwells in thee!</span><br>
<span class="line">Th’ Almighty Light which you reflect,—</span><br>
<span class="line">Th’ Almighty Soul, it dwells in me!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">2.</span><br>
<span class="line">My breath will mingle with the air,</span><br>
<span class="line">To ashes turn this mortal frame,</span><br>
<span class="line">Then turn, my soul, recall each deed,</span><br>
<span class="line">Each action done of praise or blame.</span><br>
<span class="line">My hours of life are almost past,</span><br>
<span class="line">And steals o’er me the hand of death,</span><br>
<span class="line">Then think, my soul, of actions done,</span><br>
<span class="line">Ere yet I yield this fleeting breath!</span></div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-120"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="120" data-folio="82" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n119_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 82">[p. 82]</button></div><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="stanza-no">3.</span><br>
<span class="line">Agni! friend of dying mortals,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-002" href="#sec-28-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup></span><br>
<span class="line">Lead me by the righteous path,</span><br>
<span class="line">Let me win thy gracious blessings,</span><br>
<span class="line">Let me shun thy mighty wrath!</span><br>
<span class="line">Lead me by the righteous path,</span><br>
<span class="line">In this last momentous hour,</span><br>
<span class="line">Reverently I bend to Agni,</span><br>
<span class="line">In him—worship Brahman’s power!</span></div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>From the Îsâ Upanishad.</em></p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-28-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-28-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">All names of the sun.</span></li><li id="sec-28-fn-002" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-28-fn-002" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">Address to Fire,—in reference to the rite of cremation, which is considered beneficial to the departed.</span></li></ol></section>
