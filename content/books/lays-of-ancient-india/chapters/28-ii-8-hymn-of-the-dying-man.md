They are the last three verses of the Upanishad from which they are translated.</p></div><h3 class="sec-h2">1.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza"><span class="sc">Sole</span> Rishi ! Pushan ! glorious Yama !<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-001" href="#sec-28-fn-001" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-001" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
God of day ! withdraw thy rays,<br>
And let me once more view thy splendour,—<br>
View it with my closing eyes !<br>
Let me once more view the light,<br>
The gladsome light that dwells in thee !<br>
Th' Almighty Light which you reflect,—<br>
Th' Almighty Soul, it dwells in me !</div></div><h3 class="sec-h2">2.</h3><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">My breath will mingle with the air,<br>
To ashes turn this mortal frame,<br>
Then turn, my soul, recall each deed,<br>
Each action done of praise or blame.<br>
My hours of life are almost past,<br>
And steals o'er me the hand of death,<br>
Then think, my soul, of actions done,<br>
Ere yet I yield this fleeting breath !</div></div><div class="page-rail-marker" id="page-120"><button type="button" class="folio-trigger" data-page="120" data-folio="82" data-scan="https://archive.org/download/laysofancientind00dutt/page/n119_w1400.jpg" aria-label="Inspect scan for page 82">[p. 82]</button></div><h4 class="sec-h3">3.</h4><div class="prose-unit"><div class="verse-stanza">Agni ! friend of dying mortals,<sup class="fn-ref"><a id="ref-sec-28-fn-002" href="#sec-28-fn-002" data-fn-target="sec-28-fn-002" role="doc-noteref">*</a></sup><br>
Lead me by the righteous path,<br>
Let me win thy gracious blessings,<br>
Let me shun thy mighty wrath !<br>
Lead me by the righteous path,<br>
In this last momentous hour,<br>
Reverently I bend to Agni,<br>
In him—worship Brahman’s power !</div></div><div class="prose-unit"><p class="prose-p"><em>From the Îsâ Upanishad.</em></p></div><section class="apparatus-zone" aria-label="Footnotes"><h2 class="apparatus-heading">Critical Apparatus &amp; Notes</h2><ol class="apparatus-list"><li id="sec-28-fn-001" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-28-fn-001" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">All names of the sun.</span></li><li id="sec-28-fn-002" class="apparatus-item" role="doc-endnote"><a class="fn-backlink" href="#ref-sec-28-fn-002" aria-label="Return to text">*.</a> <span class="fn-body">Address to Fire,—in reference to the rite of cremation, which is considered beneficial to the departed.</span></li></ol></section>
