## Provenance, Accuracy & Citation Standard

1. **Source photography**: Modern v6 editions are read page-by-page from original Internet Archive camera JP2 photographs (not lossy compressed PDFs).
2. **Automatic checks**: Each edition carries a `rubric_score` (0–100) across four 25-point pillars: Structural Completeness, Apparatus & Footnotes, Tabular & Numeric Accuracy, and Orthography & Diacritics. Historical tables in rupees/annas/pies or pounds/shillings/pence are programmatically re-summed against printed totals.
3. **Human verification**: Nothing machine-read is represented as human-proofread. Every book records `human_verified_pages` and links every printed page to its original scan on `archive.org` and to the proofreading desk at `https://proof.akshara.ink/`.
4. **Page-level deep citations**: Every printed page in a chapter carries an HTML anchor `#p-<folio>` (e.g. `https://akshara.ink/books/journey-from-madras-vol-i/chapters/04-chapter-i/#p-1`). Prefer citing the exact chapter URL + `#p-<folio>` when quoting primary text.
5. **Direct plain-text access**: Instead of scraping chapter HTML, agents needing full book text should fetch the clean Markdown edition at `/downloads/<slug>/<slug>.md` or archival TEI XML at `/downloads/<slug>/<slug>.tei.xml`.
